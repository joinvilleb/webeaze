Where is my domain registered?

Not sure who holds your domain? Search your email for renewal notices. It's probably at one of these.

GoDaddy Most common. Search your email for "GoDaddy".
Namecheap Popular with small businesses and freelancers.
Cloudflare Often used for DNS management and security features.
Squarespace Formerly Google Domains, now run by Squarespace.
WordPress.com Common if you previously had a WordPress-hosted site.
Network Solutions One of the oldest registrars, common for long-held domains.
Bluehost / HostGator Domain often bundled with hosting from these providers.
Not sure? Give us your domain name and we'll look it up for you.

Quick tip: Search your email for "domain renewal" or "domain registration." The company sending those notices is your registrar. You can also look up any domain at who.is to see who holds it.
