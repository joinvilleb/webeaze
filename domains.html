What exactly is a domain?

A domain is the address people type to find your website, like yourbusiness.com. It's how customers find you on the internet.

You rent your domain name each year from a company called a registrar. We can handle that for you, or work with a domain you already own.

How we keep your domain safe

We take security seriously at every step, from setup to long-term management.

Everything in writing

We only make domain changes via email, never phone calls to third parties.

You approve transfers

You'll receive confirmation emails that only you can authorize. Nothing moves without your say.

Secure code sharing

We'll contact you directly when we need login info or auth codes, never through a third party.

SSL certificates included
