What exactly is a domain name?

A domain is the address people type to find your website, like yourbusiness.com. It's how customers find you online.

You rent a domain annually from a company called a registrar. Common ones include GoDaddy, Namecheap, and Cloudflare. We can work with whatever you have, or get you a new one if you're starting fresh.

Connecting a domain to your website just means updating a couple of settings, called DNS records, to point your domain to where your site lives. We handle all of that.
