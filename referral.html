Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the referral program.

Who can participate in the referral program? +
Anyone can participate. You do not need to be a WebEaze client to refer businesses. Friends, family, colleagues: if you know someone who needs a website, you can earn $50 when they sign up.
What counts as a successful referral? +
A successful referral is when someone you refer signs up for any paid plan and remains an active client for at least 7 days. We verify all referrals during their intake process.
How do I submit a referral? +
Fill out our referral form with their name and contact info, or have them mention your name when they reach out. We track referrals for 90 days from first contact, so submit early to lock it in.
How and when do I get paid? +
After your referral's first week as a client clears, we will send your $50 within 7 business days. You choose how you want to receive it: Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or Zelle.
Is there a limit to how many people I can refer? +
No limit at all. Refer as many businesses as you want and earn $50 for each one. Some people have referred multiple clients and collected several hundred dollars in total.
What if they do not mention my name? +
Submit the referral form before they reach out. That way we have a record on file and we will match it up when they contact us. If there is ever any confusion, just get in touch and we will sort it out.
Where can I view the full referral program terms? +
You can view the complete terms as outlined in our Terms of Service.
