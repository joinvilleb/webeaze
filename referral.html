Common questions

Everything you need to know about the referral program.

Does anyone can refer, or just existing clients? +
Anyone can refer a business and earn $50 cash, you don't need to be a WebEaze client. Existing clients have the extra option to swap that $50 for 1 free month on their plan, but only through August 1, 2026.
What counts as a successful referral? +
A successful referral is someone you referred who signs up for any paid plan and stays an active client for at least 7 days. We verify all referrals during their intake process.
How do I submit a referral? +
Fill out the referral form with the business's name and contact info, or have them mention your name when they reach out. We track referrals for 90 days from first contact, so submit early to lock it in.
When do I get my reward? +
After your referral's first week as a client clears, we'll reach out so you can pick your reward. Cash is sent within 7 business days via your preferred app; a free month credit is applied to your next billing cycle.
Is there a limit to how many people I can refer? +
No limit. Refer as many businesses as you want and earn the reward for each successful signup.
What if they don't mention my name? +
Submit the referral form before they reach out. That way we have a record on file and we'll match it when they contact us. If there's ever any confusion, just reach out and we'll sort it out.
Where can I view the full program terms? +
You can view the complete terms in our Terms of Service.
