Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the referral program.

Who can participate in the referral program?
Anyone! You don't need to be a WebEaze client to refer businesses. Friends, family, colleagues - if you know someone who needs a website, you can earn.
What counts as a successful referral?
A successful referral is when someone you refer signs up for any paid plan and remains an active client for at least 7 days. We verify all referrals during their intake process.
How do I submit a referral?
Just fill out our referral form with their name and contact info, or have them mention your name when they reach out. We track referrals for 90 days from first contact.
How and when do I get paid?
After your referral's first week as a client clears, we'll send your $100 within 7 business days. We pay via Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or Zelle - your choice.
Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?
No limit! Refer as many businesses as you want. Each successful referral earns you $100. Some of our top referrers have earned hundreds of dollars.
What if they don't mention my name?
Submit the referral form first with their info - that way we have a record. We'll match it up when they reach out. If there's ever a question, just contact us and we'll sort it out.
