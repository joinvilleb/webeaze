How fees are applied

Nothing on this page is ever charged automatically or without your knowledge.

How billing works

  • Every fee requires your explicit approval before we invoice or start work. We'll always confirm the exact amount first.
  • One-time fees are invoiced separately from your subscription and appear as their own charge.
  • Monthly add-ons are added to your regular billing amount starting the next billing cycle after activation.
  • Invoices are sent by email with a secure payment link. We don't store card information.
  • If you purchase directly through a Stripe link above, the fee is processed immediately and work begins once payment is confirmed.

Fair use

  • Fair-use limits exist to protect service quality for all clients, not to restrict normal use. Most clients never come close to them.
  • Heavy image editing fees may be waived for minor or occasional overages at our discretion. We'll always tell you before applying one.
  • If your usage consistently approaches limits, we'll reach out to discuss options before applying any fees.

Other things to know

  • Requests not listed here are quoted based on scope. We'll always send a written estimate before starting any unlisted work.
  • Fees may be updated periodically. We'll notify you at least 30 days in advance of any change that affects your account.
  • All fees are subject to our Refund Policy. Generally, completed work is non-refundable.
  • Questions about a fee? Send us a message and we'll clarify before you commit to anything.
