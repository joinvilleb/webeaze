How fees are applied

No fee on this page is ever charged without your prior approval.

How billing works

Every fee requires your approval before work begins. One-time charges appear as separate invoices from your subscription. Monthly add-ons are added to your regular billing starting the next cycle after activation.

Invoices are delivered by email with a secure payment link. We do not store card information. Purchases made directly through the Stripe links on this page are processed immediately, and work begins once payment clears.

Fair use

Fair-use limits protect service quality for all clients, not to restrict normal work. Most clients never reach them.

If usage consistently approaches a limit, we will reach out before any fee is applied. Minor overages for heavy image editing may be waived at our discretion.

Other things to know

Requests not listed here are quoted individually based on scope, with a written estimate provided before any work begins.

Fees may be updated with at least 30 days' notice to affected accounts. All fees are subject to our Refund Policy; generally, completed work is non-refundable.

Questions about a specific fee? Send us a message before committing.
