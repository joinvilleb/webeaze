How fees are applied

Billing information

All fees require your approval via invoicing. One-time charges appear as separate invoices from your plan. Monthly add-ons are added to your regular billing starting the next cycle after activation (ex: your plan is $169/month and Google Business Profile is $79/month, so your plan is now $248/month).

Invoices are sent via email with a secure payment link. Card information is never stored and purchases must be made through the secure link.

Fair-use limits

Fair-use limits are designed to protect service quality for all clients, not to restrict normal work. Most clients never approach these limits. Learn more about this in the Help Center.

Custom quotes & fee updates

The minimum fee for custom quotes is $99.00 USD.

Requests not listed here are quoted after a review. A written estimate is provided with a secure payment link. Fees may be updated with at least 30 days' notice. Fees follow the Refund Policy.
