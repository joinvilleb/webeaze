What's Included vs. Extra

Your monthly plan covers day-to-day needs. Here's a quick reference.

Included in Your Plan

  • Unlimited content updates
  • Text, link & image changes
  • Layout tweaks on existing pages
  • Security updates & backups
  • SSL certificate & hosting
  • Standard domain (with credit)

Costs Extra

  • Additional website pages
  • E-commerce functionality
  • Custom integrations & forms
  • Booking system setup
  • Rush or after-hours requests
  • Additional domains

Billing & Policies

How fees are applied and billed.

How Billing Works
  • All fees require your approval before we bill.
  • One-time fees are billed separately from your subscription.
  • Monthly add-ons are added to your regular billing amount.
  • Invoices are sent by email with secure payment links.
Fair Use
  • Fair-use limits only apply when usage impacts other clients.
  • Heavy Edit fees may be waived for minor or occasional overages.
  • We will always notify you before applying any usage-based fees.
Good to Know
  • Unlisted requests are quoted based on complexity.
  • Fees may change — we will notify you in advance.
  • All fees are subject to our Refund Policy.
