What's Included vs. What Costs Extra

Your monthly plan covers most day-to-day needs. Below is a quick reference.

Included in Your Plan

  • Unlimited content updates
  • Basic image resizing & optimization
  • Text changes, link updates
  • Layout tweaks within existing pages
  • Security updates & backups
  • SSL certificate & hosting
  • Standard domain (with credit)

Costs Extra

  • Additional website pages
  • E-commerce functionality
  • Custom integrations & forms
  • Booking system setup
  • Rush or after-hours requests
  • Heavy image editing batches
  • Additional domains

Billing & Policies

Important information about how fees are applied and billed.

How Billing Works
  • All fees require your approval before we bill.
  • One-time fees are billed separately from your monthly subscription.
  • Monthly add-ons are added to your regular subscription amount.
  • Invoices are sent to your email with secure payment links.
Fair Use Policy
  • Fair-use limits only apply in cases of excessive usage that impacts our ability to serve other clients.
  • Heavy Edit fees may be waived for occasional or minor overages at our discretion.
  • We will always communicate before applying any usage-based fees.
Good to Know
  • Custom requests not listed here are quoted based on complexity.
  • Fees are subject to change. We will notify you of any updates in advance.
  • We reserve the right to decline requests that do not align with our services.
  • All fees are subject to our Refund Policy.
