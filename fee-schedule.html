Plan Coverage

What your plan already covers

Before reviewing the fee schedule, it helps to know what's included at no extra cost. The fees below only apply to requests that go beyond your plan's scope. When in doubt, just ask and we'll let you know before doing anything.

Included in every plan

  • Unlimited content updates (text, images, links)
  • Layout adjustments on existing pages
  • Bug fixes and error corrections
  • Security updates and regular backups
  • SSL certificate and hosting
  • Standard domain (with credit on Growth plan)
  • Mobile and browser compatibility checks

Costs extra

  • Additional website pages beyond your plan
  • E-commerce store setup
  • Custom integrations and third-party tools
  • Booking and scheduling system setup
  • Same-day or after-hours turnaround
  • Additional registered domains
  • Footer credit removal

Billing Policies

How fees are applied and billed

We keep billing straightforward. Nothing on this page is ever charged automatically or without your knowledge. Here is how the process works.

How billing works

  • Every fee on this schedule requires your explicit approval before we invoice or start work. We'll always tell you the exact amount first.
  • One-time fees are invoiced separately from your subscription and appear as their own charge on your payment method.
  • Monthly add-ons are added to your regular subscription billing amount starting the next billing cycle after activation.
  • Invoices are sent by email with a secure payment link. We don't store card information.
  • If you purchase directly through a Stripe link, the fee is processed immediately and work begins once payment is confirmed.

Fair use

  • Fair-use limits on updates and support hours exist to protect service quality for all clients, not to restrict normal use. Most clients never come close to them.
  • Heavy image editing fees may be waived for minor or occasional overages at our discretion. We'll always tell you before applying one.
  • If your usage consistently approaches limits, we'll reach out to discuss whether a plan upgrade or custom arrangement makes sense before applying any fees.

Other things to know

  • Requests not listed here are quoted based on scope and complexity. We'll always send a written estimate before starting any unlisted work.
  • Fees may be updated periodically. We'll notify you at least 30 days in advance of any change that affects your account.
  • All fees are subject to our Refund Policy. Generally, completed work is non-refundable.
  • Questions about a fee? Send us a message and we'll clarify before you commit to anything.
