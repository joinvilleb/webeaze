Refund Policy

Payments are non-refundable once project setup begins. This includes the initial design work, configuration, and any time invested in understanding your business.

Refunds are not issued for:

  • Partial months of service
  • Canceled subscriptions (no prorated refunds)
  • Completed work, even if you change your mind
  • Requests that exceed the original agreed scope

Before you commit, we provide a free mockup so you can see exactly what you're getting. If the mockup doesn't work for you, there's no obligation to proceed.

Read full Refund Policy

Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your plan at any time. There are no cancellation fees or penalties. It's month-to-month, and we believe in earning your business every month.

When you cancel:

  • Hosting and maintenance stop immediately
  • No refunds for the current billing period
  • You'll receive a confirmation email right away
  • Your files can be requested within 30 days (if account is in good standing)

Good standing means your account has been active for at least 12 consecutive months with no outstanding balance. Accounts active for less than 12 months may incur a file export fee. See the Fee Schedule for details.

Support Policy

All plans include ongoing support for website maintenance, updates, and minor changes. Requests are submitted through the Website Request page and handled in order of priority.

Essential Email support only
Growth Email, Chat, Phone, Video

Higher-tier plans receive faster response times when possible.

What's considered "out of scope":

  • Redesigning more than 30% of your website
  • Building complex automations or integrations
  • Custom development beyond standard CMS capabilities
  • Major structural changes to site architecture
  • Content creation or copywriting (unless added as paid service)

Out-of-scope requests are quoted separately before work begins.

Billing & Fees

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. Your card is charged on the same date each month, and you can manage your billing details through the Client Portal.

If a payment fails, we'll retry automatically. After approximately 7 days of failed payments, your account may be suspended until the balance is resolved. We'll always try to contact you first.

Additional fees such as file export fees or late payment fees, are outlined in the Fee Schedule.

Domain & Hosting Policy

WebEaze handles DNS and hosting setup for all plans. Coverage varies by plan:

  • Essential: Setup included. Hosting costs paid by client; free if using our standard GitHub Pages setup.
  • Growth: Setup included plus a $50 credit toward hosting costs.

For hosting and domain costs to be covered by WebEaze:

  • Website must be hosted via GitHub Pages (our standard setup)
  • Domain must be registered through Namecheap (our preferred registrar)
  • Domain must be a standard extension (.com, .net, .org). Premium or specialty domains may not be covered.

If you already have a domain elsewhere, we'll connect it to your new site at no extra charge. You remain responsible for renewing that domain with your existing registrar.

Add-On & Automation Policy

Add-ons (like extra pages, booking integrations, or custom features) are billed separately unless they're already included in your plan's scope.

Automation projects (email sequences, CRM integrations, custom workflows) require a separate Scope of Work document. Once automation work begins, it is non-refundable.

We'll always discuss pricing and scope before starting any add-on or automation work. No surprise charges.

Credits & Transfers

Platform Credits: If you recently paid for hosting elsewhere (within the past 2 months) and can provide receipts, we may issue a credit toward your WebEaze subscription.

Site Transfers: If you're transferring a site from another provider, any transfer fees charged by that provider are your responsibility unless we specifically agree to cover them in writing.

File Exports: When you cancel, you can request your website files within 30 days. If your account is in good standing (12+ months active), there's no fee. Otherwise, see the Fee Schedule.
