Referral FAQs

Refer a business to WebEaze and earn $50 when they sign up.

The reward

Anyone who refers a business that becomes a paying client earns $50 in cash, sent within 7 days by Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or Zelle. Refer as many businesses as you like and earn for each one.

If you are an existing WebEaze client, through August 1, 2026 you can swap your $50 for one free month on your plan instead (worth $169 on Essential or $249 on Growth), credited to your next billing cycle. After August 1, the standard $50 cash reward continues for everyone.

We have successfully paid out over $1,500 in referral bonuses to date.

How to get started
  1. Share WebEaze. Tell a business owner who needs a website about us. Send them to webeaze.io, or have them mention your name when they book a call.
  2. They sign up. When they become a paying client, we verify the referral. We track referrals for 90 days from first contact, so submit early.
  3. You get rewarded. After their first week as a client clears, we reach out. Pick $50 cash, or swap it for a free month if you are a client.
Who should you refer?

Think about the business owners you know who could use a better online presence:

  • Local business owners. Restaurants, salons, contractors, and cleaning services, anyone who needs to be found online in their area.
  • New entrepreneurs. Friends starting a business who need their first professional website but do not know where to begin.
  • Outdated websites. Anyone whose site looks stuck in 2012 and is losing customers because of it.
  • The DIY-frustrated. Owners tired of fighting with Wix or Squarespace who just want someone reliable to handle it all.
Not sure what to say?

Keep it natural. A few examples you can use or make your own:

  • "I know a company that builds and manages websites for small businesses. They handle everything: updates, hosting, SEO. You just pay monthly and never think about it."
  • "Have you heard of WebEaze? They do websites for businesses like yours. No big upfront cost, just a flat monthly fee. Want me to send you their info?"
  • "Check out webeaze.io. They do a free discovery call so you can talk through your vision before committing to anything."

Common questions

Can anyone refer, or just existing clients?

Yes. Anyone can refer a business and earn $50 cash. Existing clients do have the opportunity to swap that $50 for one free month, through August 1, 2026. You do not need to be a WebEaze client.

What counts as a successful referral?

Someone you referred who signs up for any paid plan and stays an active client for at least 7 days. We verify all referrals during intake.

How do I submit a referral?

Fill out the referral form with the necessary information, or have them mention your name when they sign up with us. We'll track referrals for up to 90 days from the first contact on either the form or their initial communication.

When do I get my reward?

After your referral's first week as a client clears, we reach out so you can pick your reward. Cash is sent out via Zelle, PayPal, Cash App, or Venmo. It's sent within 7 days by your preferred method.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No. You can refer as many businesses as you want and earn the reward for each successful signup.

What if they don't mention my name?

That's fine. You can submit the referral form before they reach out so we can know who referred them.

Where can I view the full program terms?

You can view the complete terms in our Terms of Service.
