Meet the Founder

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Miami and the founder of WebEaze. I started this company because I saw too many small businesses struggle with overpriced agencies, confusing DIY builders, and websites that never quite felt right.

My focus is on building intuitive, high-performance websites and the systems that power them. I specialize in frontend development, UI design, analytics integration, and automation - to turn business needs into clean, scalable solutions.
