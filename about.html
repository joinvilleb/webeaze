We're your business's digital team, not just a one-time hire.

WebEaze is a web presence company built for small business owners who are great at what they do but don't have time to deal with websites, SEO, Google rankings, or any of the tech that comes with it. We handle all of it: website, optimization, and ongoing management, under one flat monthly price. No agencies to chase, no surprise invoices, no tech headaches. Just a business that's easier to find and harder to ignore.

Built with a reason.

Too many small businesses are invisible online. Not because they aren't great at what they do, but because no one ever gave them the right support. We're here to change that.

Hi, I'm Billy Joinville

Founder, WebEaze

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Miami. I started WebEaze because I kept seeing the same problem: small businesses were paying thousands upfront to agencies, getting a site built, and then left completely on their own with no updates, no support, no results.

I specialize in frontend development, UI design, SEO, analytics, and automation. Through WebEaze I've shipped 70+ production websites, working directly with business owners to build a digital presence they're genuinely proud of, one that actually brings in clients. We now serve businesses across Delaware, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and beyond.

WebEaze stays small by choice. I'd rather know every client's business deeply and deliver real, measurable results than scale up and become just another agency that disappears after launch.
