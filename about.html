Meet the founder
Billy Joinville
Founder, WebEaze

A Computer Science graduate from the University of Miami, I started WebEaze in 2024 after taking a web design course because I kept seeing the same problem where small businesses were paying thousands upfront to agencies, getting a website built, and left completely on their own.

I wanted to change that, creating a service that would give small business owners a website they could be proud of, without the stress, tech headaches, or surprise bills.

Our team is very small, with my other team member Jason Wakin who shares my vision for delivering exceptional web experiences for our clients.

We're small by choice. I'd rather know every client's business deeply and deliver real, measurable results than scale up and become just another agency that disappears after launch. I'm happy you're here reading this.
