Hi, I'm Billy Joinville
Founder, WebEaze

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Miami. I started WebEaze because I kept seeing the same problem: small businesses were paying thousands upfront to agencies, getting a site built, and then left completely on their own with no updates, no support, no results.

I specialize in frontend development, UI design, SEO, analytics, and automation. Through WebEaze I've shipped 70+ production websites, working directly with business owners to build a digital presence they're genuinely proud of. We now serve businesses across Delaware, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and beyond.

WebEaze stays small by choice. I'd rather know every client's business deeply and deliver real, measurable results than scale up and become just another agency that disappears after launch.
