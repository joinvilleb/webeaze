Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Billy Joinville

Founder, WebEaze

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Miami. I started WebEaze because I kept seeing the same problem: small businesses were paying thousands upfront to agencies, getting a site built, and then left on their own — no updates, no support, nothing.

I specialize in frontend development, UI design, analytics, and automation. Through WebEaze I've shipped 40+ production websites, working directly with business owners to build something they're genuinely proud of. We now serve clients across Delaware, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and beyond.

WebEaze stays small by choice. I'd rather know every client's business and deliver real support than scale up and become just another agency.

"Every small business deserves a website that makes them proud, backed by someone who actually cares about their success."
Billy Joinville  ·  Founder, WebEaze
