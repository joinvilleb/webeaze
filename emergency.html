FAQ

Common Questions

How fast do you respond?
Urgent repairs are prioritized immediately. Most issues are addressed within hours, depending on severity and complexity.
What counts as an emergency?
A site outage, broken checkout, form failure, hacked website, or anything preventing your site from functioning normally for visitors.
Is this included in my plan?
Yes. Emergency fixes are included for all WebEaze clients with an active subscription at no extra charge.
Can you help with sites you didn't build?
Absolutely. If you have an existing site hosted elsewhere, we still handle emergency fixes. Learn more about our website management services.
Do I need to be a client to get help?
Emergency support is included with all active WebEaze subscriptions. If you're not a client yet, check out our plans to get started. We'd love to have you.
What if my site needs more than a quick fix?
We'll stabilize your site first, then work with you on a plan for larger fixes or improvements. You'll never be left with a half-finished solution.
