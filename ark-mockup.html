Who We Are

More Than a Tutoring Center

The ARK Educational Resource Center exists to close the opportunity gap. We provide after-school support, mentorship, and community programs for students in Laurel, DE because every child deserves a space to thrive.

Academic Support
One-on-one and group tutoring across all grade levels and subjects.
Mentorship
Long-term academic and career goal-setting with mentors who care.
Family Resources
Tools and support for parents to stay engaged no matter their schedule.
Community Programs
Annual events, backpack giveaways, and neighborhood initiatives.
