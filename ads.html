FAQ

Common questions about ad management

How much should I spend on ads?
We recommend starting with at least $200-$500 per month in ad spend. This budget goes directly to Google or Meta, not to us. We will help you determine the right budget based on your industry, goals, and local competition.
How quickly will I see results?
Most clients see their first leads within the first week of ads going live. However, the real magic happens in months 2-3 as we gather data and optimize your campaigns for better and better performance.
Do I need a WebEaze website to use this?
This add-on is available to active WebEaze plan subscribers and one-time web project clients. If you are not a client yet, start with a free mockup and we will get you set up with both a great website and effective ads.
Can I run ads on both Google and Facebook?
Yes. Your $149/mo management fee covers both platforms. We will recommend the best platform mix based on your business type and goals. Many clients see great results running both simultaneously.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. There are no contracts and no commitments. You can cancel anytime. We earn your business every month by delivering results, not by locking you in.
What is included in the monthly report?
Your report includes impressions, clicks, click-through rates, conversions, cost per lead, and overall return on ad spend. We also include our analysis of what is working, what we are testing, and our recommendations for the next month.
