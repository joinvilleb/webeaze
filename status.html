Recent Updates

Changes to Domain and Hosting Coverage Jan 3, 2026

To ensure we provide an all-in-one service, we've updated our domain and hosting coverage policy. Effective January 3, 2026, all new and renewing clients will have their domain costs covered directly through WebEaze as part of their plan. Hosting coverage will also be included if we're building and hosting the website via GitHub Pages. This change doesn't impact existing clients with active domain or hosting credits. Read full details in our Help Center.

Total Access Support Jan 3, 2026

To enhance your support experience, we've introduced Total Access Support for Growth and Elite plan clients. This bundled feature includes options for Google Meet sessions, Live Chat, and Phone support, ensuring you can reach us in the way that suits you best.

Switch to Google Meet for Live Support Nov 25, 2025

To make your client experience even easier and more reliable, we're transitioning from Zoom to Google Meet for all live support sessions beginning December 1, 2025. Moving forward, our Zoom Live Support benefit will be renamed Google Meet for naming consistency.

Current Events and Service Impacts Nov 7, 2025

We're aware of ongoing events occurring in the United States and their impact on small businesses. If you're at risk of missing a payment or need to pause your service, please contact us as soon as possible to discuss options. We are here to help.

Plan Upgrade Savings for WSP clients Oct 27, 2025

Legacy clients can upgrade to our Essential or Growth plans before November 30 to secure reduced pricing. You will receive an email if you're eligible for this special offer.

Google Business Profile Integration Launched Oct 15, 2025

We've added Google Business Profile integration to our Growth and Elite plans. Now we can help manage your business listing on Google through a simple website request.
