Recent Updates

Website Setup Package Ending May 31, 2026 Policy May 13, 2026

All original Website Setup Package agreements are ending on May 31, 2026. This applies to clients who signed up under our earlier one-time package before WebEaze moved to a subscription model.

Clients who have already upgraded to an Essential or Growth plan are not affected and their service continues normally.

Clients who have not yet responded to our emails or taken action to upgrade will have their website support, domain management, and hosting coverage revoked as of the deadline. We have sent multiple email notifications and, where possible, physical letters to every affected client. If your contact information has changed or you have not received any communication from us, please reach out immediately at support@webeaze.io.

If you are on the Website Setup Package and want to continue with WebEaze, you need to select a current plan before May 31, 2026. Plans start at $169 per month with no long-term contract required. Read the full transition details or view current plans.

Elite Plan Discontinued Policy Mar 1, 2026

We have simplified our plan offerings to two options: Essential and Growth. The Elite Plan has been discontinued as of March 1, 2026.

Existing Elite plan clients are not affected and will continue receiving their current level of service without any interruption or change to their billing. No action is needed from current Elite clients.

For clients who were considering the Elite Plan, the Growth plan includes the same core features, and additional services are available as add-ons. Read the full details in the Help Center.

Introducing One-Time Web Projects Feature Feb 18, 2026

WebEaze now offers One-Time Web Projects for clients who need a website built without a monthly subscription. This option is designed for businesses that want a professional site delivered once, with no ongoing commitment.

One-Time Projects are available at multiple scopes depending on your needs. After the project is delivered, clients have the option to add a subscription plan if they decide they want ongoing support, updates, and hosting management going forward. Learn more about One-Time Web Projects.

AI Chatbot & Ad Management Add-Ons Now Available Feature Feb 18, 2026

Two new optional add-ons are now available to any WebEaze client on a current plan.

AI Chatbot: A custom AI assistant trained specifically on your business, your services, and your frequently asked questions. It is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week directly on your website to answer visitor questions and capture leads. Learn more about the AI Chatbot.

Ad Management: We create and manage Google Ads and Facebook or Instagram ads on your behalf for a flat monthly fee. This includes ad copy, targeting, and ongoing optimization. Learn more about Ad Management.

Changes to Email Communications Announcement Feb 4, 2026

We have stopped sending monthly newsletters to clients and contacts. Going forward, updates about WebEaze services, policies, and features will be posted here on this page and sent as individual emails only when there is something genuinely important to communicate.

This change was made to keep communications focused and relevant. You will not receive routine emails from us unless something directly affects your account or service. If you have questions about anything related to your website or plan, the Help Center and this status page are the best places to start.

Updated Domain & Hosting Coverage Policy Policy Jan 3, 2026

As of January 3, 2026, domain registration and GitHub Pages hosting are covered as part of all new and renewing WebEaze subscription plans. Clients no longer need to manage or pay for these separately as long as their plan is active.

This change does not affect existing clients who have active domain or hosting credits. Those credits will continue to apply for the remainder of their term. Read the full policy details in the Help Center.

Introducing Total Access Support Feature Jan 3, 2026

Growth plan clients now have access to Total Access Support, a bundled support tier that includes Google Meet video sessions, Live Chat through the website, and direct Phone support. This means you can reach us through whichever channel works best for you on any given day, without needing to schedule in advance for every question.

Total Access Support was introduced in response to feedback from clients who wanted faster, more flexible ways to get help. Rather than being limited to email or a scheduled call, Growth plan clients can now get real-time answers during business hours through chat or phone, and screen-share sessions through Google Meet for anything more involved.

If you are on the Essential plan and would like to upgrade to Growth to take advantage of Total Access Support, you can view current pricing at pricing.html or reach out to us at support@webeaze.io.

Switching to Google Meet for Live Support Announcement Nov 25, 2025

Starting December 1, 2025, all live support sessions at WebEaze moved from Zoom to Google Meet. This applies to all scheduled screen-share appointments and any live video support included with the Growth plan. Any Zoom links previously shared with clients are no longer active.

Google Meet was chosen because most clients already have a Google account, which means there is nothing to download and no account setup required to join a session. Sessions can be joined directly from a browser link, which reduces friction for clients who are not familiar with third-party meeting software.

When a live support session is scheduled, you will receive a Google Meet link by email. If you have any trouble joining, contact us at support@webeaze.io before your appointment and we will assist you.

Support for Clients Affected by Government Shutdown Announcement Nov 7, 2025

During the period of federal government disruptions in late 2025, WebEaze made accommodations available to clients who were directly affected by reduced income, delayed payments, or uncertainty related to the shutdown. We recognize that unexpected financial pressure can make it difficult to maintain recurring service costs, and we wanted to make sure clients did not lose their website or service continuity because of circumstances outside their control.

Clients who reached out during this period were offered payment deferrals, temporary pauses, or adjusted billing arrangements on a case-by-case basis. No client who communicated with us about their situation was dropped or penalized for the impact of the shutdown.

If you are ever facing a situation where you are at risk of missing a payment or need to discuss your plan, please contact us at support@webeaze.io as early as possible. We are always willing to work with clients who reach out proactively rather than going silent.

Plan Upgrade Savings for Legacy WSP Clients Announcement Oct 27, 2025

In October 2025, WebEaze extended a limited-time offer to all clients who had originally signed up under the Website Setup Package. Eligible clients were given the opportunity to transition to either the Essential or Growth subscription plan at a reduced introductory rate, as a reward for being early WebEaze clients and to make the switch as easy as possible.

All eligible clients were contacted directly by email with the details of their specific offer and instructions on how to complete the upgrade. The offer period ran through November 30, 2025. Clients who completed their upgrade during that window received the discounted rate applied to their first billing cycle on the new plan.

This offer has now closed. Clients who did not upgrade during the offer period can still transition to a current plan at the standard rate. If you have questions about your current status or want to discuss your options, reach out to us at support@webeaze.io.

Google Business Profile Integration Added Feature Oct 15, 2025

Growth plan clients can now request Google Business Profile management as part of their existing subscription. This includes tasks such as updating business hours, adding photos, responding to reviews, and keeping your profile information accurate and consistent with your website. Requests are submitted through the standard Website Request form, the same way website updates are handled.

Google Business Profile is one of the most important tools for local visibility. Keeping it accurate and active improves how your business appears in Google Search and Google Maps results, which directly affects how many new customers find you. Growth plan clients who have not yet claimed or connected their profile are encouraged to do so and submit a request so we can assist with the setup.

If you are on the Essential plan and would like access to Google Business Profile management, you can upgrade to Growth at any time. Visit pricing.html to compare plans or contact us at support@webeaze.io with any questions.

Welcome to the Support Center Announcement Sep 10, 2025

This page launched in September 2025 as the central location for all WebEaze service updates, policy changes, and support announcements. Before this page existed, clients had to rely on email to stay informed about changes to their service. The Support Center gives you a place to check at any time, without waiting for a message to arrive.

The page is organized with the most recent updates at the top. Each entry includes a category badge, the date it was posted, and a full description of what changed and why. Updates cover everything from new features and pricing changes to policy updates and scheduled maintenance. Older entries are archived and available through the button at the bottom of the page.

We recommend bookmarking this page so you can check back whenever you want to know what is happening across WebEaze. If you ever have a question that is not addressed here, the Help Center is the best place to look, and you can always reach us directly at support@webeaze.io.
