Recent Updates

Elite Plan Discontinued Mar 1, 2026

We've simplified our offerings to two plans — Essential ($169/mo) and Growth ($249/mo). The Elite Plan has been discontinued. Existing Elite clients will continue receiving service without interruption. Read the full announcement.

Introducing One-Time Web Projects Feb 18, 2026

We now offer One-Time Web Projects — no monthly plan required. Choose from Basic ($149), Standard ($299), or Full Build ($449). Learn more.

AI Chatbot & Ad Management Add-Ons Feb 18, 2026

Two new add-ons are now available. AI Chatbot — a custom assistant trained on your business, available 24/7. Learn more. Ad Management — we create and manage Google and Facebook/Instagram ads for you. Learn more.

Changes to Email Communications Feb 4, 2026

We've stopped sending monthly newsletters. Important updates are now posted here and emailed as they happen — reducing inbox clutter and keeping things relevant.

Updated Domain & Hosting Coverage Policy Jan 3, 2026

All new and renewing clients have domain and GitHub Pages hosting covered as part of their plan. This doesn't affect existing clients with active credits. Full details in the Help Center.

Introducing Total Access Support Jan 3, 2026

Growth plan clients now have Total Access Support — Google Meet, Live Chat, and Phone support bundled together so you can reach us however works best for you.

Switching to Google Meet for Live Support Nov 25, 2025

We've moved from Zoom to Google Meet for all live support sessions starting December 1, 2025.

Support for Clients Affected by Government Shutdown Nov 7, 2025

If you're at risk of missing a payment or need to pause your service due to economic impact, contact us as soon as possible and we'll work something out.

Plan Upgrade Savings for Legacy WSP Clients Oct 27, 2025

Legacy clients were able to upgrade to Essential or Growth at reduced pricing before November 30. Eligible clients were contacted by email.
