One-Time Project Your current features and benefits
What's Included in Every Tier
  • Professional design & development work: Custom work built to your brand and brief.
  • Mobile-responsive implementation: Your project works correctly on all screen sizes.
  • Quality assurance testing before delivery: We review everything before handing it over.
  • Up to 3 minor revisions within 14 days of delivery: Small text, image, or layout tweaks at no extra charge.
Project Tiers
  • Basic: Single-page scope. Text edits, image swaps, minor fixes on one existing page. Typically delivered in 3–5 business days.
  • Standard: Up to two pages. Content changes, layout adjustments, and section redesigns across existing pages. Typically 5–7 business days.
  • Full Build: Complete new website, up to 3 pages plus a contact page, designed from scratch. First draft typically within 7–14 business days.

For pricing on each tier, see the Fee Schedule.

Not Included
  • Ongoing maintenance or future updates after delivery
  • Hosting, backups, or security monitoring
  • Priority support, performance checks, or strategy sessions
  • Google Business Profile setup or management
  • Domain registration (invoiced separately if required)
  • Content creation or copywriting
