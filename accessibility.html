Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to access our website, and our clients' websites, with ease. This statement outlines our commitment to accessibility, the standards we follow, testing methods, known limitations, and how to get help.


Last reviewed: August 2025

Our Commitment

WebEaze is dedicated to making our website and websites we build reasonably accessible for all users of all abilities. We follow widely recognized best practices and incorporate accessibility considerations into our design, content, and development workflows.

Our goal is to accessibility over time and address issues as they are identified.

What We Do

We implement practical, industry-standard accessibility practices including:

  • Using meaningful HTML structure for headings, lists, labels, and landmarks.
  • Providing descriptive alt text guidance and accessible naming for links and buttons.
  • Designing for clear keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Applying recommended color contrast ratios where feasible.
  • Building layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • Respecting reduced-motion preferences when possible.
  • Encouraging accessible media use (e.g., captions or transcripts for core WebEaze videos when published).

Third-Party Tools

Some functions rely on external services (e.g., billing, scheduling, embeds, analytics). We choose vendors that prioritize accessibility when available, but we cannot control or guarantee the accessibility of third-party tools. If any external widget creates a barrier, we will offer an alternative method of completing the task.

Limitations and Ongoing Work

Despite our efforts, some accessibility issues may still be present, including:

  • Third-party widgets that may not fully meet accessibility standards.
  • Older or legacy client sites built before our latest design updates.
  • Client-provided content that does not meet recommended accessibility guidelines.
We continually work to improve accessibility across our properties and review this statement periodically.

Compatibility

We test with current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) and perform spot-checks using assistive technologies (e.g., screen readers and keyboard-only navigation) to ensure a functional experience.

Testing & Monitoring

Accessibility checks are integrated into our design and development workflows, including:

  • Manual keyboard walkthroughs
  • Screen reader spot-checks.
  • Automated audits
  • Content guidance for alt text, headings, and content clarity.

These represent reasonable efforts rather than a guarantee of full WCAG conformance.

Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you experience an accessibility barrier, we want to help. You may contact us through any of the following:

  • Contact Us form
  • Email: support@webeaze.io
  • Phone: (347) 377-2408 (9:00am-5:00pm Monday-Friday, except for holidays and closures)
  • Help Center: webeaze.tawk.help

To help us investigate, please share:

  • the page URL
  • your browser or device
  • any assistive technologies used
  • a brief description of the issue

Alternative Formats

Upon request, we can provide accessible or simplified versions of key documents like invoices and plan summaries. We'll do our best to provide a format that works for you.

Continuous Improvement

We review and update this statement periodically as we enhance our accessibility practices.
