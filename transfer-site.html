Policy

Website Content & Domain Transfer

How website files and domain management are handled after your plan is canceled.

Summary

Subscribed for 12+ months?

Website files can be provided and domains may be transferred.

Subscribed <12 months?

$99 transfer fee applies or files are removed.

Strict timelines

Deadlines apply once the cancellation email is sent to you.

Plans active for 12 months or longer

If your WebEaze plan has been active for at least 12 months:

  • Website files will be provided within 30 days of receiving your cancellation email.
  • Domain management can be transferred if requested within 30 days.

Plans active for less than 12 months

If your plan has been active for fewer than 12 months:

  • Website files will be permanently deleted within 2 working days of cancellation.
  • Domain management will be canceled within 2 working days of cancellation.
  • A $99 site transfer fee must be paid within that window if you wish to receive website files and transfer domain management.
File and transfer requests must be submitted by email within the stated timeframes. No exceptions. We're not responsible for anything that gets lost/changed/damaged when contents are transferred from us to you.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, please reach out to us here. Ensure your requests are sent within the specified timeframes as our team cannot accommodate late submissions.

Final note

We truly appreciate the opportunity to have worked with you and wish you the best of luck in your future endeavors.
