Special Arrangements

Custom Plans & Exceptions

How student pricing, hourly arrangements, or temporary setups work.

▾

Some clients use student pricing, hourly arrangements, or other custom setups. These follow the same process as standard plans but may have different billing terms.

After around four website requests on a custom arrangement, we may recommend moving to a standard plan for consistency and cost efficiency. Any change is always discussed and confirmed in writing.

Guidelines

Quick Expectations

Practical guardrails so you know how we operate.

▾
  • Turnaround: Requests are handled in queue based on complexity and plan level.
  • Revisions: Please send one consolidated list per request for efficient handling.
  • What’s included: Standard design and updates; advanced tools or custom systems may require separate scoping.
  • Communication: Use the Website Request page for anything related to website updates.
  • Billing: Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled under the Terms of Service.
  • Accommodations: Any custom arrangement is always confirmed in writing.
