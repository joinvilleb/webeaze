Recommended for you

Hosting included

For most clients, hosting is completely covered in your monthly plan. We deploy on GitHub Pages and manage your domain through Namecheap. No separate hosting bill, ever.

  • $0 in additional hosting costs
  • 99.9% uptime, global CDN
  • Free SSL certificate included
  • Fully managed — zero work on your end
  • Domain credit included in most plans
Recommended for you

Bring your own platform

If you're already on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or another platform — we work with what you have. You pay the host directly at their published rate, no markup.

  • No markup on hosting fees — you pay them directly
  • Same plan pricing regardless of your host
  • We handle all setup and ongoing management
  • Migration assistance available if switching
  • Works with all major platforms
