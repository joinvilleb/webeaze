How Hosting Works

Your plan includes hosting. Here's what that means.

Default Setup

Hosting Included

For most clients, hosting is completely covered in your monthly plan. We deploy your site on GitHub Pages and manage your domain through Namecheap. No separate hosting bill.

  • $0 in hosting costs to you
  • 99.9% uptime
  • SSL certificate included
  • Global CDN, fast load times
  • Fully managed, zero work on your end

Premium Platforms

If you need WordPress, e-commerce, or already have a preferred platform, we'll work with it. You pay the host directly at their published rate.

  • No markup on hosting fees
  • You pay the host directly
  • We handle setup and management
  • Same plan pricing regardless of host
  • Migration assistance available

GitHub Pages (hosting) + Namecheap (domains)
