What's included in every project
  • Professional design and development built to match your brand, not a template.
  • Mobile-responsive implementation that works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • QA review before delivery so you receive something polished and ready to use.
  • Up to 3 minor revisions within 14 calendar days of delivery at no extra charge.
What's not included

Since there's no ongoing plan, the following aren't part of a one-time project:

  • Ongoing maintenance or future updates after delivery
  • Hosting, backups, or security monitoring
  • Priority support, performance reports, or Google Meet sessions
  • Domain registration for full builds (added to your invoice separately if needed)

If you want someone managing the site long-term after delivery, you can subscribe to any monthly plan. See the 14-day offer below.

How it works
1

You pay and we confirm

Choose the tier that fits and complete checkout. You'll get a payment confirmation from Stripe. Make sure your email address is correct since we'll use it to follow up with next steps.

2

We reach out within 1 business day

Our team contacts you to confirm project details and request anything we need to get started: your existing site URL, brand assets, or a description of the changes needed.

3

We get to work

Once we have what we need, work begins. Timelines vary by tier: Basic in 3 to 5 days, Standard in 5 to 7 days, Full Build first draft in 7 to 14 days. Complex projects or delays on your end may extend the timeline.

4

You review and request revisions

We send you a preview for review. You can request up to 3 minor revisions within 14 calendar days of delivery as long as they fall within the original scope.

The 14-day window starts when you're notified the project is complete, not when you purchased.

Minor revisions include: text changes, image swaps, color or font adjustments, typo or broken link fixes.

Revisions outside the original scope (new sections, new features, layout redesigns) are invoiced separately.

5

We deliver or go live

For Basic and Standard projects, we apply the changes to your existing site and notify you when complete.

For Full Builds, we handle domain setup, QA testing, and launch. You'll receive all files and credentials at delivery. After handoff, we don't retain access unless you subscribe to a plan.

Subscribe after delivery

After the 14-day window, the standard setup fee applies. To subscribe, submit a request through our Website Request page and let us know which plan you'd like. We'll send a custom link that applies both discounts.

Eligibility and policies
Existing WebEaze clients aren't eligible for one-time projects. If you're already on a monthly plan, submit updates through the standard request process. One-time project pricing can't be applied as a credit or discount toward a subscription.

Refunds follow our Refund Policy. Have a project that doesn't fit the standard tiers? Contact us and describe what you need. We can quote larger or more complex projects outside of the standard tiers.

Questions

Not sure which tier is right?

If you're not sure whether your project is a Basic, Standard, or Full Build, reach out before purchasing and we'll help you figure out the right scope. It's better to ask up front than to be invoiced for extra pages after the fact.
