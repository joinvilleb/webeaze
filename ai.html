Common questions, straight answers

Everything you need to know before adding AI to your website.

What if the chatbot cannot answer a question?

When the chatbot encounters a question outside its training, it politely lets the visitor know and collects their name, email, and message so you can follow up personally. No lead gets lost.

Will it make things up or say something wrong?

No. The chatbot only answers based on the information you provide. It does not make up facts, go off-topic, or say anything you have not approved. You are always in control.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

Not at all. We handle everything: building the chatbot, installing it on your site, and maintaining it. You just tell us about your business and we take care of the rest.

What happens if my business info changes?

Most chatbots are live within a few business days. Once you provide your business details, we move fast. You will review and approve everything before it goes live.
