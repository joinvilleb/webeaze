What Happens Next

We move quickly but keep things simple and transparent.

1. Onboarding

We confirm your details and begin structuring your initial layout. From this point, communication is conducted via email.

2. Content & Access

Submit a Website Request with content and requested credentials.

3. Review & Launch

We'll send a live preview for feedback and revisions. Most launches go live within 1-2 weeks.

Quick To-Dos (Takes ~5 Minutes)

1. Check your email

We've sent you a welcome email with plan information, Scope of Work details, and next steps. Ensure support@webeaze.io is saved in your contact book.

2. Look over the timeline

Review milestones and how we reach your launch date. We may send you an email to collect more information. Reply to it promptly.

3. Sit tight

We're working on your website. If we need anything, we'll let you know. Sites are typically launched within 1-2 weeks.

Tips for a Smooth Launch

Send clear, high-quality images. Your logo in SVG or PNG format works best.

List your services in bullet form and define the key action you want visitors to take.

If your business runs on appointments or quotes, share your usual availability and policies.

Reply to emails we send regarding site setup. The faster you reply, the faster the launch timeline.
