New: AI Search

Your business needs to show up in AI answers, not just Google results.

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews for a local service recommendation, your business needs to be in the answer. That requires a different kind of optimization.

  • FAQ schema so AI can extract clear answers
  • Entity markup that defines your business for AI systems
  • Speakable markup for voice and AI-driven answers
  • llms.txt file that communicates directly with AI crawlers
  • Business descriptions optimized for AI comprehension
