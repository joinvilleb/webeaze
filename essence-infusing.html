Why it's hard

The recipe isn't the hard part.

Hot sauce, herbal extracts, lip balm — they share one challenge: controlling heat, time, and movement precisely. Do it by hand and you're mostly guessing.

  • 01You end up watching the heat instead of letting it run.
  • 02Timing and stirring rely on approximation, not clear signals.
  • 03It's easy to miss the moment something isn't being handled right.
  • 04Small changes end up having outsized effects on the result.

ELOH holds those variables steady — so repeatable and preserved stop being at odds.
