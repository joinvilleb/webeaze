Cookie Policy
How WebEaze uses cookies, what data is collected, and how to manage your preferences.
Last updated: April 2025

We use cookies to keep the site working and to understand how it's being used. We do not sell your data. Questions? Email legal@webeaze.io.

What We Use

Essential
WebEaze / Stripe

Required for the site to work. Covers security and payment processing. These cannot be turned off.

Analytics
Google Analytics 4

Helps us see which pages are visited and where traffic comes from. All data is aggregated and anonymized.

Marketing / CRM
HubSpot

Tracks form submissions so we can follow up on consultation requests. Nothing is shared with advertisers.

Preferences
WebEaze (your browser)

Saves things like which help articles you have read. This data stays on your device and never leaves it.

How to Turn Them Off

Go to your browser settings and search for "Cookies" to manage or delete them. Heads up: turning off cookies may break contact forms and some personalization features on this site.

Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
Firefox: Settings > Privacy and Security
Safari: Preferences > Privacy
Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Have a concern?

Email our legal team and we will get back to you within 3 business days.
