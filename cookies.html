Cookie Policy
How WebEaze uses cookies, what data is collected, and how to manage your preferences.
Last updated: April 2025

Cookies help our site work correctly and allow us to understand how visitors use WebEaze so we can improve it. We do not sell your data. If you have concerns about how your data is used, email us at legal@webeaze.io and we will respond within 3 business days.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language or whether you have already filled out a contact form. Cookies do not give us access to your computer or any personal information beyond what you share with us.

We use a mix of first-party cookies (set directly by WebEaze) and third-party cookies (set by services we use, such as Google Analytics and HubSpot).

Cookies We Use

Type Provider Purpose
Essential WebEaze / Stripe Required for the site to function. Includes session security and payment processing via Stripe. These cannot be turned off.
Analytics Google Analytics 4 Helps us understand which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and where traffic comes from. Data is aggregated and anonymized.
Marketing / CRM HubSpot Tracks form submissions and contact activity so we can follow up on consultation requests. No data is sold or shared with advertisers.
Preferences WebEaze (localStorage) Stores lightweight preferences in your browser, such as which help articles you have read and your plan interest, so we can personalize your experience. This data never leaves your device.

How to Manage Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. The steps vary by browser:

Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
Firefox: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and Site Data
Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

Note that disabling cookies may affect how parts of this site work, including contact forms and the Help Center personalization features.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time as our services or applicable laws change. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of our site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions or Concerns?

If you object to any of the cookies described above or have questions about how your data is handled, reach out to our legal team. We review every request and respond within 3 business days.
