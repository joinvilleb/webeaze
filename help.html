New to WebEaze?

Start here

Here's how the whole process works, from sign-up to going live.

Step 01
Choose a plan
Pick Essential or Growth depending on how many pages and how much support you need. No contract required.
Step 02
Gather what you need
Logo, brand colors, a rough page list, photos, and your business info. Rough notes are fine.
Step 03
We build your draft
Most first drafts are ready within 7 to 14 days after you submit your content. We'll email you when it's ready to review.
Step 04
Review, revise, go live
Up to 3 revision rounds before launch. You approve the final draft, we connect your domain and publish it.
