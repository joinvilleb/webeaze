Our Story

A parlor for
the senses

Tucked into the heart of Wynwood, Lagniappe is Miami's most beloved wine bar — a place that feels like it was lifted from a New Orleans jazz-age parlor and set down among the palm trees.

Over 150 wines and beers. Live music every single night. Grilled food and charcuterie designed to be shared. No reservations — just show up.

"Feel like you've stepped into an old New Orleans parlor from the jazz age — live nightly music, 150-plus wines, and a cozy interior with an expansive light-strung outside listening yard."

— Zagat
