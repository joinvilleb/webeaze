How it works

The careful part, handled.

ELOH runs the infusing, melting, mixing, and activation from one touchscreen, built for precise, low-temperature preparation across culinary, wellness, and craft.

The difference

By hand, or handled.

Doing it by hand

You manage every variable

  • Watching the heat instead of letting it run
  • Timing and stirring by approximation
  • Easy to miss when something's off
  • One batch great, the next one flat
With ELOH

The system holds it steady

  • Indirect radiant heat, set and held automatically
  • Timing tuned to what you're making
  • Automated mixing, no hovering
  • Repeatable results, batch after batch
Start to finish

How a session goes.

1

Choose your program

Pick what you're making on the touchscreen. It loads the right temperature, timing, and motion. No settings to figure out.

2

Add your ingredients

Your botanicals and a carrier: oil, honey, butter, whatever the recipe calls for. Close it up and start.

3

Let it run

Indirect radiant heat, timing, and mixing are handled automatically. The fragile compounds are protected while you do something else.

4

Use what you made

Pour, jar, or bottle it. Repeat the exact result next time, or change one thing and explore.
