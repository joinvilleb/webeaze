What's covered

What happens during your performance check

Each round, a real member of our team goes through your site. Here's what every check covers.

SEO titles & descriptions

We review and update your page meta titles and descriptions to keep your site visible and competitive in search results.

Link check

Every link on your site is checked to make sure it's working. Broken links are flagged and fixed before visitors run into them.

Account notes review

We check your account notes for anything pending. If there's something we can do for you, we'll take care of it or reach out.

Speed & performance

We run a quick check on load times to catch anything that might be slowing your site down for visitors.

Security & SSL

We verify your SSL certificate is active and your site is loading securely — no warnings for your visitors.

Summary email

Once complete, we send you a plain-English email summarizing exactly what was done and when your next check is scheduled.

Year-round support

We're on it between checks too.

The bi-monthly check is a scheduled review, but our team is actively watching your site all year. Requests, fixes, and updates happen whenever you need them.

  • Unlimited update requests, anytime
  • Same team who knows your site
  • Urgent issues prioritized at no extra cost
  • 24–48hr turnaround on most requests
