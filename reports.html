What We Check

What happens during your performance check

Each round, a real member of our team goes through your site and takes care of what needs attention. Here's what every check covers.

SEO titles & descriptions

We review and update your page meta titles and descriptions to keep your site visible and competitive in search results.

Link check

Every link on your site is checked to make sure it's working. Broken links are flagged and fixed before visitors run into them.

Account notes review

We check your account notes for anything pending or outstanding — if there's something we can do for you, we'll take care of it or reach out.

Speed & performance

We run a quick check on load times and performance to catch anything that might be slowing your site down for visitors.

Security & SSL

We verify your SSL certificate is active and your site is loading securely — no warnings for your visitors.

Summary email to you

Once complete, we send you a plain-English email summarizing exactly what was done and when your next check is scheduled.

Between Checks

We're on it between checks too.

The bi-monthly check is a scheduled check-in, but our team is actively watching your site in between. Requests, fixes, and updates happen year-round.
