FAQ

Questions about unlimited management

Is it really unlimited? What's the catch?

No catch. Unlimited means unlimited. Text changes, photo swaps, new pages, layout adjustments—send as many requests as you need. We don't have "fair use" policies or hidden limits. Our business model is built around keeping you happy, not nickel-and-diming you for every change.

What platforms do you support?

WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom HTML/CSS, and most other platforms. If your site exists, we can probably manage it. Check our supported platforms page for the full list.

How fast do you complete requests?

Most requests are completed same-day, often within a few hours. For urgent issues (site down, broken forms, etc.), we prioritize immediately. We don't charge extra for fast turnaround—it's just how we operate.

Can you improve my existing design?

Absolutely. If your site looks dated or isn't converting well, we can refresh layouts, modernize visuals, and improve the user experience. Sometimes we even offer a full redesign at no extra cost when you join a management plan.

What if I need something complex?

Custom functionality like booking systems, e-commerce features, or integrations may require additional one-time development costs. We're transparent about this upfront—no surprises. Day-to-day updates and improvements are always included.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts, no cancellation penalties. We earn your business every month. If you're not happy, you're free to leave. (But our clients tend to stick around.)
