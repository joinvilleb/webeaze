You may have questions.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade your plan at any time with prorated billing. However, you cannot downgrade your plan for 12 months from the plan start date.

Do you migrate existing sites?

Yes. By selecting our Growth plan, we can refresh your design, improve performance, and migrate hosting without losing content.

How soon can we start?

Most projects start right away and launch in 7-14 days depending on content readiness.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards. Payment is processed securely via Stripe.

Why is there a setup fee?

Yes. A one-time setup fee of $299 applies to monthly Essential and Growth plans to cover initial design, development, and launch costs. To avoid the setup fee, consider our yearly plans where the setup fee is included in the annual price.
