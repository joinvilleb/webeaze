Common Questions

Quick answers to help you decide

What does "unlimited updates" mean?

You can request as many content changes as you need: text, photos, pricing, hours, services. No extra fees. The only exception is major redesigns or new features, which we'd discuss first.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. You tell us what you want in plain English. We handle all the technical work. You never need to log into anything or learn any software.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or commitments. Cancel any time with no penalties. We believe in earning your business every month.

What if I already have a website?

We can work with most existing sites. We'll review what you have and recommend whether to improve it, migrate it, or start fresh. Your first consultation is always free.

How long until my site is live?

Most websites go live within 5 business days of content approval. You'll see a custom mockup within 48 hours of your first call.
