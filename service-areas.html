Why we list areas at all

Our work is done remotely so we can build a website for a business anywhere in the USA. We list specific areas because local search is genuinely local. Showing up for "landscaper near me" in Baltimore depends on things that are specific to Baltimore: which directories matter there, how your competitors have set up their Google Business Profiles, and what customers in that market actually search for.

The pages above reflect that. They're not the same page with a city name swapped in. If you're in one of these areas, start there. If you're not, the service is identical, we just have less to say about your local market on day 1.
