Common questions

What platforms do you support?

WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom HTML, and most other platforms. Check our supported platforms page for details.

Can you improve my existing design?

Yes. If your site looks outdated or isn't performing well, we refresh layouts, improve visuals, and modernize the overall feel. Sometimes we offer a full rebuild at no extra cost when you join a plan.

Is it really unlimited updates?

Yes. Text changes, new photos, layout tweaks, adding pages. Send requests whenever you need them. All included in your monthly plan.

Can you migrate my hosting?

Yes. If you want faster, more reliable hosting, we handle the entire migration. Your site stays live the whole time.

What if I need something urgent?

We prioritize urgent requests. If something critical needs fixing, let us know and we'll handle it right away.
