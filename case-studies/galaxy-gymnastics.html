The challenge

Galaxy Gymnastics had outgrown its old website. It was hard to read on a phone, buried the class schedule, and made parents call or email just to find a time or sign up. For a gym running programs for hundreds of kids across every age, that friction cost enrollments and ate up staff time answering the same questions.

They needed a site that looked as professional as their coaching, made schedules and sign-ups effortless, and could be updated the moment classes or events changed, without hiring a developer every time.

What we did

We rebuilt the entire site from scratch around how parents actually use it, then took over hosting, updates, and everything technical.

A custom, modern design

Built for the brand and fast on every phone, so the site looks as good as the gym feels.

Clear class schedule

The full schedule front and center, so families find a time in seconds instead of calling.
