The challenge

The owner, Chelsea Lahey, expressed concerns of overpaying their previous web designer while their website hasn't been working for them the way they wanted. It was hard to read on a phone, buried the class schedule, and loaded too slow for parents to use. For a gym running programs for hundreds of kids across every age, that friction cost enrollments, and discovery among locals in Salisbury.

They needed a site that looked as professional as their coaching, made schedules and sign-ups effortless, seamless updates as requested without paying more, with potential to be discovered by new families in the area. That's where we came in.

What we did

We rebuilt the entire website from scratch, using parts of the old website that we found were successful. We also focused on the user experience, making sure the site was easy to use and provided a good experience for parents.

A custom, modern design

Built for the brand and fast on every phone, so the site looks as good as the gym feels.
