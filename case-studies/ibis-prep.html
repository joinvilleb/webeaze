The challenge

Ibis Prep was growing on word of mouth, but its website didn't match the quality of the tutoring. It was dated, hard to navigate, and made parents work to understand the programs or get in touch. For a business built entirely on trust, the site was leaving every referral to do the convincing on its own.

They needed a site that looked as credible as their results, made programs easy to understand, and turned interested parents into booked families without any back and forth.

What we did

We rebuilt the site around how parents actually choose a tutor, then took over hosting, updates, and everything technical.

Clear program pages

Each program broken out so families quickly find the right fit for their student.

Tutor bios

Real faces and credentials up front, so parents trust who they're hiring.

Simple intake form

Families go from interested to scheduled in a couple of taps, no phone tag.

Mobile-first design

Looks sharp on the phone parents actually reach out from.
