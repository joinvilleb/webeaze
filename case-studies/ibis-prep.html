The challenge

Andrew Dennis came to us because their website was confusing to navigate and not effectively converting visitors into customers. For a business built entirely on trust, the site was leaving every referral to do the convincing on its own.

They needed a site that looked as credible as their results, made programs easy to understand, and turned interested parents into booked families without any back and forth.

What we did

We took initiative, and rebuilt the site around how parents actually choose a tutor, then took over hosting, updates, and everything technical.

Clear program pages

Each program broken out so families quickly find the right fit for their student.

Tutor bios

Real faces and credentials up front, so parents trust who they're hiring.

Simple intake form

Families go from interested to scheduled in a couple of taps, no phone tag.

Mobile-first design

Looks sharp on the phone parents actually reach out from.

Local SEO

Optimized so families searching for tutoring and test prep find Ibis Prep.

Unlimited updates

New programs, tutors, or seasonal test-prep pushes? We handle it, usually same or next day.
