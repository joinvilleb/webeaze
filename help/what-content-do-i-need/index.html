What content do I need to provide?

Last updated: 05-27-2026    4 min read

On this page

  • Page 1 of 3: Your contact info
  • Page 2 of 3: Your domain setup
  • Page 3 of 3: Your brand and business info
  • After you submit

After subscribing, you'll fill out our Content Submission Form. It takes about 15 minutes and has 3 pages. Here's a full walkthrough so you know what to have ready before you open it.

Page 1 of 3: Your contact info

Basic info about you and your business. You'll need:

  • First and last name
  • Email address — use the same email you used when you signed up for your plan
  • Phone number — we won't spam or text you unsolicited
  • Business name
  • Industry (optional) — choose the category that best fits your business, or leave it blank

Page 2 of 3: Your domain setup

We need to know where your website address is coming from. Think through this before you open the form:

  • Your domain situation — one of three options:
    • I already own a domain
    • I want to transfer my domain to WebEaze
    • I need to purchase a new domain
  • How you'd like to give us access (only applies if you already own a domain or want to transfer one):
    • Grant us a temporary login to your domain registrar
    • Have us guide you step-by-step
    • Schedule a call so we can walk through it together
  • Existing website? — whether you have a current site and whether you want to keep content from it or start fresh
  • Additional domain notes (optional) — if you want to buy a new domain, this is where you tell us the URL you want. We'll purchase it as part of your plan.

Page 3 of 3: Your brand and business info

This is the most important page. It's how we understand your business so we can design something that actually fits. Have these ready:

  • Logo file(s) (optional) — SVG or PNG preferred. If you don't have one yet, leave it blank and let us know in the notes. See What if I don't have a logo yet?
  • Brand colors — hex codes like #FF5733 are ideal, but plain descriptions like "navy blue, white, and orange" work too. See What if I don't have brand colors?
  • Brand vibe — select all that apply from the list: Professional & Corporate, Modern & Clean, Bold & Confident, Traditional & Trustworthy, Friendly & Approachable, Premium & Luxury, Natural & Eco-friendly, or Rugged & Hardworking
  • Instagram link (optional) — your business Instagram profile URL if you have one
  • Additional business info — a short write-up covering your tagline or slogan, what your business does, the services you offer, where you serve customers, and what makes you different. Bullet points are totally fine. This is the most important field on the whole form, so take your time with it.
Tip: Write your business description in a notes app before opening the form so you can copy and paste it in. It'll save time and you won't lose it if the page refreshes.

After you submit

Once you submit the form, we'll review everything and follow up by email if we need any clarification before we start building. Most first drafts are ready within 7 to 14 days from the time we receive your content.
