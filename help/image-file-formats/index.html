What image and file formats should I send?

Last updated: 05-23-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • Photos and images
  • Logos and branding
  • Documents
  • How to send files

Sending the right file types upfront saves time and ensures everything looks sharp on your site. Here's what works best for each type of content.

Photos and images

  • Best formats: JPG/JPEG for photos. PNG for anything that needs a transparent background (logos, icons, badges). WebP is ideal if you have it.
  • Minimum size: At least 1200px wide for full-width sections. At least 800x800px for square or product images. Profile photos should be at least 400x400px.
  • File size: We'll compress everything during the build, so don't worry about large files. Send the highest quality version you have.
  • What not to send: Screenshots from a phone at low resolution, heavily compressed JPEGs, or images that have already been resized down from a small original.

Logos and branding

  • Best formats: SVG (ideal, scales to any size without quality loss), PNG with a transparent background, or AI/EPS files from a designer.
  • JPEGs with a white background aren't ideal for logos but we can work with what you have. If you don't have a logo file yet, see I don't have a logo yet.

Documents

  • Best format: PDF. Try to keep files under 5MB if possible.
  • If you're sending a menu, price list, or brochure, let us know whether you want it embedded on the page, linked as a download, or both.

How to send files

Attach files by using the client portal. Share share a Google Drive or Dropbox link in your message if you don't have a file to attach.
