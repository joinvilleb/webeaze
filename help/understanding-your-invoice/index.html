Your invoice is processed through Stripe, our secure payment provider.

What you might see on your invoice

Your Plan
Your recurring subscription fee for website maintenance, hosting, and more
Setup & Onboarding Fee
One-time $199 charge for initial website design and launch. Added to 1st invoice only
Additional Domain Fee
Applied for domain renewals or setup/management of additional domain name
Add-Ons, Fees
Optional services as described in our Fee Schedule

Billing cycle

Your billing date is set when you first subscribe. For monthly plans, you'll be charged on the same date each month. For annual plans, you'll be charged once per year on your subscription anniversary.
