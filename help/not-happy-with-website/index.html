What if I'm not happy with my website?

Last updated: 04-25-2026    1 min read

On this page

  • Revision rounds
  • What if revisions don't solve the issue?
  • Refund policy

Your satisfaction is what keeps us going. If your website isn't what you expected, let us know right away so we can fix it.

Revision rounds

After the first draft of your website is ready, you'll receive a preview link (if applicable) for you to review. You can reply and let us know what needs to be adjusted or added. Each plan includes 3 revision rounds before launch. After launch, requests fall under your plan's scope.

What if revisions don't solve the issue?

If the website still doesn't meet your expectations, send us an email. We'll review and work with you to find a solution.

Refund policy

Refunds follow our Refund Policy. If we're unable to deliver what was agreed upon, we'll work with you to make it right.
