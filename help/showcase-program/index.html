Can my website be featured on the WebEaze samples page?

Last updated: 05-30-2026    1 min read

Yes. If you're an active WebEaze client and you're proud of your website, we'd love to feature it. Our Samples page is where potential clients browse real work we've built, and being included gives your business a little extra visibility.

What being featured means

  • Your website is shown to everyone who visits our Samples page
  • Visitors can click through directly to your live site
  • It's completely optional and free

How to request it

Submit a request through your client portal and mention you'd like to be featured. We'll take a look and reach out to confirm a screenshot and any details.
