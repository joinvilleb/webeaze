Understanding your website leads

Last updated: 08-30-2026    4 min read

On this page

  • The two kinds of lead
  • Where to find your leads
  • Follow up in one tap
  • Other activity
  • Your daily lead summary
  • Growth and Essential plans
  • I don't see any leads

Your website is one of your best salespeople. Every time someone reaches out through it, we count that as a lead and show it to you in your portal.

The two kinds of lead

Not everything a visitor does leaves you someone to reply to, so your Leads page keeps the two apart.

Messages are people who left their details. A contact form gives you their name, phone, email and what they asked for, so you can answer them directly. These are the ones worth acting on, and they are what the big number on your Leads page counts.

Taps are the other signals we pick up: someone tapping your phone number to ring you, tapping your email address, or tapping a booking or quote button. They tell you somebody was interested, but they carry no name and no number, so there is nobody to write back to. You will find these grouped under Other activity at the bottom of the page.

Why we split them. A tap on your phone number is a footprint, not a message. Counting it next to a real inquiry made your total look bigger than the number of people you could actually call back.

Where to find your leads

Open your portal and select > Leads. You will also see a quick count on your home page.

The number at the top is your messages this month, the ones with details you can reply to. Just underneath, we tell you how many taps came in as well.

Every message shows who reached out, which page they were on, whether they were on a phone or a computer, where they found you, and when. That is usually enough to know who to ring first.

Follow up in one tap

For every message, the portal gives you a head start:

  • Tap the phone number or email to call or message them right away.
  • Select Draft a reply and we write a warm, ready-to-send response tailored to what they asked. Copy it, or open it straight in your email with one tap, then edit and send.
  • As you work through them, set each one to Contacted, Won or Lost, so you can always see at a glance who is still waiting on you.
Speed wins. The faster you follow up, the more likely you are to win the customer.

Other activity

Open Other activity to see every tap on your phone number, email address or booking buttons, with the page it happened on and the time.

There is nothing to reply to here, but the time is the useful part. If someone tapped your number at 8:41 and you have a missed call around then, that is the one worth chasing.

Your daily lead summary

Once a day after working hours, we'll email you a summary of the leads your website brought in that day.

If you're on a Growth plan, you get a ready-to-send reply in that email, so you can follow up without even opening the portal.

Growth and Essential plans

Both plans include a Leads overview.

If you're on a Growth plan, you also receive the full lead inbox which includes:

  • Each person's name, phone, email, and message
  • One-tap follow-up
  • AI-drafted replies

I don't see any leads

Lead tracking is switched on for you when your site goes live if we built it.

You may not see the Leads tab available in your portal if you joined with an existing website. Submit a request to get your leads set up.
