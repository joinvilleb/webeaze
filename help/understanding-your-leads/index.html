Understanding your website leads

Last updated: 08-10-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • What counts as a lead
  • Where to find your leads
  • Follow up in one tap
  • Your daily lead summary
  • Growth and Essential plans
  • I don't see any leads

Your website is one of your best salespeople. Every time someone reaches out through it, we count that as a lead and show it to you in your portal.

What counts as a lead

A lead is any real sign of interest from a visitor on your site. We track these automatically:

  • Contact form messages, including the name, phone, email, and message they sent.
  • Clicks to call, when someone taps your phone number to ring you.
  • Clicks to email, when someone taps your email address.
  • Booking or quote clicks, when someone heads to your booking, quote, or consultation page.

Where to find your leads

Open your portal and select > Leads. You will also see a quick "Leads this month" count on your home page.

Each lead shows who reached out, how they found you, and when, so you know exactly who to follow up with first.

Follow up in one tap

For every lead, the portal tells you the best next step and gives you a head start:

  • Tap the phone number or email to call or message them right away.
  • Select Draft a reply and we write a warm, ready-to-send response tailored to how they reached out. Copy it, or open it straight in your email with one tap, then edit and send.
  • Once you have reached out, select Mark contacted so you always know who still needs a reply.
Speed wins jobs. The faster you follow up, the more likely you are to win the work, so reach out while you are still fresh in their mind.

Your daily lead summary

Once a day after working hours, we'll email you a summary of the leads your website brought in that day.

If you're on a Growth plan, you get a ready-to-send reply in that email, so you can follow up without even opening the portal.

Growth and Essential plans

Both plans see how many leads your bringing in. The full lead inbox, with each person's name, phone, email, message, one-tap follow-up, and AI-drafted replies, is part of the Growth plan.

I don't see any leads

Lead tracking is switched on for you when your site goes live if we built it.

If you came in with an existing website, you may not see the leads available in the portal. Submit a request to get your leads set up.
