What is the AI Chatbot add-on?

Last updated: 02-18-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What is the AI Chatbot?
  • What it does
  • Platform compatibility
  • How to get started

We offer an AI-powered chatbot that can be added to any WebEaze plan for $79/month, added to your subscription plan or invoice.

What is the AI Chatbot?

A custom AI assistant trained on your specific business — your services, hours, pricing, and FAQs. It answers visitor questions 24/7, captures leads and sends them to your email, and matches your website's design. We set it up and keep it updated as your business changes.

What it does

  • Answers customer questions instantly, 24/7
  • Trained on your specific business info
  • Captures leads and sends them to your email
  • Matches your website's design and branding
  • We update it as your business changes

Platform compatibility

The AI chatbot works with our list of supported platforms here. If your website is on a supported platform, we can add the chatbot. The frontend widget is installed on your website, while the AI processing is handled by our servers. This means static sites like GitHub Pages work perfectly.

How to get started

Submit a request and let us know you'd like to add the chatbot. Since we know your business, we'll set it up and get it running within 3-5 working days.

Note: Add-on services are available to active WebEaze plan subscribers and one-time web project clients. See What happens when I cancel an add-on? for full details on how cancellation works.
