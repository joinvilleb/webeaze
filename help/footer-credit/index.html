Why does my website have a footer credit?

Last updated: 04-11-2026    2 min read

As part of our website design and support services, we include a small credit line at the bottom of the websites we build or manage. This typically reads something along these lines:

  • "Built by [Website Builder] and maintained by WebEaze."
  • "Website designed by [Website Builder] and maintained by WebEaze."
  • "Website designed & Maintained by WebEaze."
  • "Designed by HTML Codex. Edited and Maintained by WebEaze."
  • "Website Maintained by WebEaze."
This footer credit is common in the web design industry and serves several purposes:
  • Recognizing Our Work: the team behind the website's creation, helping others discover a trusted provider for their own projects.
  • Support Reference: signaling visitors and your team who to contact for future support, changes, or improvements.
  • Business Growth: helps us continue to grow through referrals and word-of-mouth, allowing us to keep offering affordable, high quality services to small businesses.

Can the credit be removed? Yes, we offer the option to remove or customize this credit upon request. There's a $149 fee to remove it as this credit helps offset our marketing costs.

If you'd like the wording of our footer credit to be changed to better fit your brand or preferences, let us know - we'll change it at no extra cost.
