Can you help me write or update the copy on my website?

Last updated: 07-16-2026    1 min read

On this page

  • What we can write or update
  • Tips for better content
  • What isn't included

Yes. Updating your website's copy is included in your plan. Send us a request and we'll handle the writing.

What we can write or update

  • Service descriptions and pricing
  • About page copy and team bios
  • Homepage messaging and calls to action
  • FAQ sections
  • Promotional or seasonal content
  • Any other text currently on your site

Tips for better content

  • Be specific about who you help and what makes you different from competitors
  • Include your service area if you're a local business
  • If you have stats or results ("200+ clients served," "5 years in business"), share them and we'll work them in

What isn't included

Blog posts, social media captions, and email marketing copy aren't part of your plan. Check out the blog add-on or newsletter management.
