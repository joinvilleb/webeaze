Who do I contact while my website is being built?

Last updated: 05-26-2026    1 min read

Your client portal is the best place to reach us while we build. Submit any questions, edits, or additional content there as a request, and you'll see a live status on each one so nothing gets missed.

You'll also get email updates at key moments in the build, including when we begin work, when your first draft is ready for review, and when your website is live. Email is great for those notifications, but for anything you need from us, the portal keeps everything tracked in one place.

Tip: We strongly recommend that you add us to your contacts list: support@webeaze.io to ensure you receive all email updates.
