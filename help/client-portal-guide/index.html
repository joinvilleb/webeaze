Welcome to your Client Portal! We hope this will make your website management experience easier.

Note: The Website Request page has been replaced with the Client Portal.

Invite and Sign In

You'll get an invite from us when your account is ready. Select "Accept invitation", set your password, and you're in.

Bookmark portal.webeaze.io and sign in with your email and password. Check "Keep me signed in" to stay logged in between visits, but only on a device that is yours.

Your password is private. We can't see it since your password is encrypted. You can reset it any time if you forget it.

To reset your password, select "Reset it" on the login page and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

Home page

Submit Requests

Select "Request a change" to to submit changes to your site. Attach files if needed. Once submitted, you'll be able to track the status of your request.

A status appears next to your request once submitted:

  • Received means we have your request.
  • In progress means we're working on it.
  • Needs info means we need a bit more detail from you. You'll see a message from us with space to reply.
  • Completed means the change is live on your site, along with a note of exactly what was done.

Your plan

The Your plan card shows your current plan status and benefits. Select "Manage plan" to manage your plan.

Website notes

For any updates or announcements related to your website, you'll find them here.

Your site report

The biggest benefit of thr portal. See your site's performance, SEO, rankings, review performance, and more. Access by selecting > Your site report.

Your milestones

Earn rewards for being a client with us. You'll see your progress and achievements here and may be rewarded with special offers. Think a free month of service. Access by selecting > Milestones.

Request history

See your complete record of past requests with us. Access by selecting > Request history.

Refer and Earn

Earn cash rewards when you refer businesses to us and they sign up. Access by selecting > Refer and Earn.

Support hours

Check our current support hours, view upcoming closures, and browse the Help Center. You can also find any applicable announcements from our team. Access by selecting > Support hours.

More to come

The portal is constantly improving. You'll see new features as they are released based on client feedback and trends.
