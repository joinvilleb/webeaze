Welcome to your Client Portal! It is your home base for managing your website. Bookmark it at portal.webeaze.io. This is a quick tour, with a link to a full guide for each part.

Note: The Website Request page has been replaced with the Client Portal.

Invite and Sign In

You'll get an invite from WebEaze when your portal is ready. Accept it, set a password, and bookmark portal.webeaze.io. Full sign-in and password help.

Your dashboard (home page)

Your home page has three cards: your live site report, a button to submit a website request, and your plan and account details. Take the dashboard tour.

Website notes

See reminders and announcements from your team about your site, and add your own notes. More on website notes.

Your site report

See live Google Search Console data plus growth tools: search performance, reviews, local competitors, and ideas to grow. More on your site report and growth tools.

Your leads

Every enquiry from your website's contact forms lands in one place, so you never miss a potential customer. More on your leads.

Milestones

Unlock badges as you go, and a few come with a surprise reward. More on milestones.

Request history

Once we finish your first request, a Request history page keeps a record of every change we make. More on request history.

Refer and Earn

Earn cash or rewards when you refer a business and they sign up. More on the referral program.

Support hours

See our current availability, upcoming holidays, recent service updates, and the Help Center. More on support hours.

More to come

The portal keeps improving. You'll see new features as they roll out, based on client feedback.
