Can you connect my email marketing list to my website?

Last updated: 05-20-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • Platforms we commonly connect
  • What we set up
  • Platform notes
  • What you'll need

Yes. Adding a newsletter or email list sign-up form to your site is a great way to grow your audience without relying on social media. We'll connect the form to whichever email platform you use so new subscribers are added automatically.

Platforms we commonly connect

  • Mailchimp — The most widely used option for small businesses. Free up to 500 contacts.
  • ConvertKit — Well suited for coaches, creators, and service providers.
  • Constant Contact — A common choice for local businesses and nonprofits.
  • HubSpot — Good if you're already using HubSpot as your CRM, since new subscribers go straight to your contact list.

Don't see your tool listed? Ask us anyway. If it has an embed form or API, we can most likely connect it.

What we set up

We'll add a styled sign-up form to your site and connect it to your email list. The form can go in your footer, a dedicated section, a popup, or a standalone page depending on what works for your layout. New subscribers are added to your list automatically.

Platform notes

  • Custom HTML and Webflow: We embed the platform's form code and style it to match your site.
  • WordPress: Most email platforms have a WordPress plugin that makes connection straightforward.
  • Wix: Wix has its own email marketing tool (Wix Email Marketing) built in. For third-party tools, we use embed code via the HTML element.
  • Squarespace: Native connections to Mailchimp are built in under Marketing settings. Other platforms use embed blocks.

What you'll need

You'll need an active account with your chosen email platform. Most have a free plan that's more than enough to start. Once you share your embed code or grant API access, we'll handle the rest.
