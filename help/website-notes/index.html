Website notes: your shared space with WebEaze

Last updated: 07-28-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • What Website notes are for
  • Where to find your notes
  • Notes from WebEaze
  • Adding your own note
  • Turn a note into a request
  • Why it helps

Website notes are a shared space in your Client Portal where we post updates about your website and account, and where you can jot down anything you want to remember or want us to know. Think of it as the running conversation that used to live in your inbox, now kept in one organized place.

What Website notes are for

Instead of emailing back and forth, we post account and website updates right in your portal. Anything we would have sent as a quick email, like a heads-up about your domain, a note that a change is live, or a reminder about something we need from you, shows up here so it is easy to find later.

Your notes and ours sit side by side, so nothing gets buried in a crowded inbox.

Where to find your notes

Open your portal at portal.webeaze.io and go to Menu > Website notes. You will see two sides: From WebEaze for our updates, and Your notes for anything you add.

Notes from WebEaze

These are updates from your web team about your specific website or account. You might see a note that a requested change is live, a reminder that we are waiting on a photo, or a heads-up about something new. They stay here for your reference, so you can always look back at what was shared and when.

Adding your own note

Use the Add a note box to write down anything you want to remember or want us to know, then select Post note. You can also attach images to a note when a picture explains it better than words.

Notes are a good place for things like "Reminder: ask about adding a holiday banner next month" or "Here is the new team photo for the About page."

Turn a note into a request

If a note is really something you would like us to do, select Turn into a request on that note. It carries the details straight into the request flow so we can get to work, and you can track its status like any other request. For more on that, see How can I request website changes?

Please keep personal information out of your notes. We can see everything you post here, so never include passwords, card numbers, or other sensitive details. If you need to share something private, contact us directly instead.

Why it helps

Keeping updates and reminders in one place means less digging through email, a clear record of what was shared, and an easy way to hand us small ideas the moment you think of them. It keeps your project organized and moving.
