Will I talk to a real person?

Last updated: 11-01-2024    2 min read

Yes. When you reach out to WebEaze, you're talking to someone who knows your website, understands your business, and can give you suggestions like no other web design agency can.

What does this mean for you?

  • Personalized support: The person helping you has context about your site, your brand, and your history with us
  • Faster resolution: Real humans can understand nuance and solve problems without scripted responses
  • Direct communication: You won't be transferred between departments or stuck in phone trees
  • Accountability: We stand behind our work because we're the ones who built and maintain your site

How to reach us

All clients can submit requests through your client portal, the fastest way to get help. Growth plan clients also have access to:

  • Google Meet sessions: Schedule a live video call for in-depth discussions
  • Live Chat: Get real-time text support during working hours
  • Phone support: Talk directly with our team when you need it

Our working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00am - 5:00pm Eastern Time, excluding major holidays. We typically respond to email requests within 1-2 working days.

Our promise: You'll never get a canned response from a bot. Every message is read and answered by someone on our team who cares about your website's success.
