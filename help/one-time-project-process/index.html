What happens after I pay for One-Time Website Help?

Last updated: 07-10-2026    4 min read

On this page

  • 1. You pay and get your portal
  • 2. We reach out within 1 business day
  • 3. We build it
  • 4. You review it
  • 5. We deliver or launch

Here is exactly what happens after you pay, step by step. You will always know where things stand, because you can follow along in your client portal.

1. You pay and get your portal

Right after checkout, Stripe emails you a payment confirmation. Use a real email address, because that is how we reach you.

Once your payment goes through, we send you an invitation to your client portal. Follow the link, create a password, and log in. Inside, you will see your project steps update as we work on your site, and you can send us a message any time if something looks off or you need help.

2. We reach out within 1 business day

A real person from our team emails you to say hello and confirm the details. We will ask for anything we need to start, like your current website link, your logo and brand colors, or a description of the changes you want.

3. We build it

Once we have what we need, we get to work. How long it takes depends on your tier:

  • Basic: usually 3 to 5 business days
  • Standard: usually 5 to 7 business days
  • Full Build: first draft usually ready in 7 to 14 business days

Bigger projects, or a slow reply on your end, can stretch the timeline a bit.

4. You review it

We send you a preview to look over. You can ask for up to 3 small changes within 14 days, as long as they fit what you originally ordered.

Small changes are things like:

  • Fixing wording, a phone number, hours, or an address
  • Swapping an image or logo
  • Tweaking a color, font size, or spacing
  • Fixing a typo or a broken link

Bigger requests are not small changes. For example: adding a new page or section, redesigning the layout, or adding a feature like a contact form or booking system that was not part of your original order. Those need a separate purchase, so just contact us and we will give you a price.

Your flat price covers the build plus those 3 rounds of small changes within 14 days. After you use your 3 rounds, or after the 14 days are up, any more changes are billed by the hour at $50.75/hour.
Good to know: the 14 days start the day we tell you your project is finished, not the day you paid.

5. We deliver or launch

For Basic and Standard projects (edits to a site you already have), we make the changes and let you know when they are live.

For a Full Build, we set up your domain, test everything, and launch it. Then we hand over all your files and logins. You own everything.

Want us to keep it running? If you would rather we host, maintain, and keep updating your site, subscribe to a monthly plan within 14 days of delivery and we will waive your first month and the $199 setup fee. Learn more.
