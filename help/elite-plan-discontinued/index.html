What happened to the Elite Plan?

Last updated: 03-09-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • What Changed
  • Why We Made This Change
  • If You're an Existing Elite Client

As of March 2026, the Elite Plan has been officially discontinued. We've simplified our offerings to two clear plans that better serve the vast majority of our clients.

What Changed

We now offer two straightforward monthly plans:

  • Essential: Up to 3 pages, unlimited updates, hosting & domain assistance, basic SEO, email support, and monthly performance reports.
  • Growth: Up to 6 pages, priority turnaround, advanced SEO, Google Business Profile management, Total Access Support, online review management, and monthly performance reports.

For projects that go beyond these plans, such as large-scale sites, e-commerce builds, or custom integrations — we still offer custom project quotes.

Why We Made This Change

We found that most businesses fit well into Essential or Growth and having a third tier created confusion. By simplifying, we can deliver a better, more focused experience at every level.

If You're an Existing Elite Client

Your service will continue without interruption. The Elite plan is considered grandfathered, meaning the plan and its benefits will remain active for you as long as you maintain your subscription. If you cancel or let it lapse, there isn't a way to re-subscribe to the Elite plan, but you can easily switch to Growth or Essential at any time.

Here's a reminder of what your plan includes:

  • Unlimited professionally designed pages
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • Unlimited content updates
  • Same-day turnaround
  • All hosting & domain costs covered
  • Monthly SEO strategy reviews
  • Total Access Support
  • Custom integrations (booking, payments)
  • E-commerce features available
  • Support schedules
  • Dedicated account manager

If you have any questions about your account, let us know.
