How do domains and hosting work?

Last updated: 07-11-2026    4 min read

On this page

  • Setting up your domain
  • Not sure where your domain is registered?
  • What happens after you reach out
  • How we keep your domain safe
  • Is hosting included?

Your domain is your web address, like yourbusiness.com. Hosting is the space where your website lives online. With WebEaze, both are handled for you. You never have to touch a DNS setting or manage a hosting account. Here is how it works, in plain terms.

The short version: tell us whether you already have a domain or need one, and we take care of the rest. Hosting and an SSL certificate (the padlock that keeps your site secure) are included with your plan.

Setting up your domain

I already have a domain

Keep it right where it is. If your domain lives at GoDaddy, Namecheap, or anywhere else, we simply point it to your new site by updating a couple of settings (called DNS). Nothing moves, you stay in control, and your email keeps working untouched. No transfer fees and no waiting. See how we connect your domain.

I want you to manage my domain for me

Prefer to hand it off completely? You can transfer your domain to us and we manage the renewals, settings, and any troubleshooting from then on. No more expiration surprises. A transfer adds a year to your registration and usually takes about 5 to 7 days.

I do not have a domain yet

Starting fresh is easy. Tell us your business name and we find the best available option, register it, and set everything up, usually the same day. Standard domains (.com, .net, .org) are covered by the domain and hosting credit included in your plan. See how domain and hosting is covered.

Not sure where your domain is registered?

How to find your registrar

Search your email for "domain renewal" or "domain registration." The company that sends those notices is your registrar. It is most often GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, Squarespace (formerly Google Domains), WordPress.com, Network Solutions, or Bluehost. You can also look up any domain at who.is to see who holds it. Still stuck? Just give us your domain name and we will look it up for you.

What happens after you reach out

The 4 steps
  1. Tell us your situation. Submit a request and let us know if you have a domain or need a new one.
  2. We send clear instructions. You get a simple email with exactly what to do, or we handle it entirely for you.
  3. Quick verification. We confirm everything is connected and your SSL certificate is active.
  4. You are live. Your website is up at your domain, ready to share.

How we keep your domain safe

Our safety rules
  • Everything in writing. We only make domain changes after email confirmation, never over the phone.
  • You approve all transfers. Nothing moves without a confirmation that only you can authorize.
  • Secure code sharing. When an authorization code is needed, we ask you directly, never through a third party.

Is hosting included?

Yes, hosting and SSL are handled

Every WebEaze site is hosted on fast, secure servers with an SSL certificate included at no extra cost. You do not need a separate hosting account or a third-party hosting bill. We take care of uptime, security, and backups as part of your plan.
