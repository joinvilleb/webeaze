Will my plan price ever change?

Last updated: 05-11-2026    1 min read

We keep our prices affordable and fair. Every September, we hold an internal review of our operational costs and current market conditions to assess whether any pricing adjustments are needed.

Pricing doesn't update often, however, when a change is warranted, here's what you can expect:

  • You'll get 30 days' notice via email before any change takes effect.
  • Existing clients usually receive special consideration, such as loyalty discounts or grace periods.
  • Your current plan price won't change midway through your billing cycle.

We work hard to keep costs stable because we value our relationship with you because that's what makes us different.
