Cancel your plan

Last updated: 07-21-2026    1 min read

We hate to see you go, however, we understand plans change. If there is anything we can do to help, contact us first.

How to cancel

Cancel your plan by logging into your client portal. Then, select Cancel Subscription. Follow the prompts to confirm cancellation. You'll receive an email confirmation once your plan is canceled.

Read the Cancellation Policy to understand the what happens after cancellation and how to transfer your website (if applicable).
