Sending us your login credentials securely

Last updated: 02-26-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • How it works
  • Your information is 100% confidential
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • When should I use this?

Some platforms don't support adding a manager or collaborator. Follow these steps instead.

How it works

Your portal allows you to securely submit your login credentials using the Site Setup wizard.

If you must submit more credentials:

  1. Email info@webeaze.io with your login details.
  2. We use your credentials only to log in and complete the requested website updates on your behalf.
  3. Once the work is done, you can change your password at any time if you wish.

Your information is 100% confidential

Any credentials you share are strictly confidential and are only viewed by WebEaze for the sole purpose of logging in and completing necessary website updates on your behalf. Your information follows our Confidentiality terms.

Two-factor authentication (2FA)

If you have two-factor authentication enabled on your account, please either temporarily disable it before we log in, or be available to provide the verification code when we attempt to access the platform.

When should I use this?

  • Your platform doesn't support adding a collaborator or manager role
  • You prefer to share credentials directly rather than navigating permission settings
  • You need us to access a specific tool or service (e.g., domain registrar, email host) that requires a direct login

Important: If you choose to share your credentials, we recommend changing your password after we complete the work for added security. Only send us credentials if requested by us. We'll never ask for your password outside of this process.

Prefer to add us as a manager instead? See How to send us website access for platform-specific instructions.
