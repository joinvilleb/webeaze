What happens if I come back later?

Last updated: 07-22-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What this means
  • Your website after cancellation
  • Your domain

If you canceled your plan before, you can come back any time. We'd love to have you back!

Reactivation is treated as a new plan, not a continuation of your previous one.

What this means

  • You'll pay a reactivation fee
  • Your previous billing date isn't preserved
  • Any billing date change you used on your previous account counts toward your one-time lifetime allowance
  • Your account history is carried over

Your website after cancellation

After cancellation, your website files are held for a period of time before being removed. Please read the Cancellation Policy for exact retention timelines.

Your domain

If we registered your domain on your behalf, reactivating your plan doesn't automatically reconnect it. We need to confirm the status of your domain to ensure it's still available.
