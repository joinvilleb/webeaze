Can I add a team or staff page to my website?

Last updated: 05-20-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What's typically included
  • Platform notes
  • Does it cost extra?
  • What to send us

Yes. A team or staff page adds a human element to your site and helps clients feel like they know who they're working with before they reach out. It works especially well for service businesses, healthcare providers, law firms, and agencies.

What's typically included

  • profile photo or professional photo
  • Name and job title or role
  • A short bio (2 to 4 sentences is ideal)
  • Optional: credentials, certifications, or LinkedIn link

Platform notes

  • Custom HTML sites: Full design control. Grid layout, card style, hover effects. We'll match it to your existing design.
  • WordPress: Team member plugins or custom post types work well. We'll pick the cleanest option for your theme to avoid plugin bloat.
  • Wix: The Team Members app or a grid layout using the built-in elements panel. Both work well for small to mid-size teams.
  • Squarespace: Profile cards and summary blocks can be styled into a clean team layout.
  • Webflow: CMS collections are the best approach so you can add or update team members yourself without a request.

Does it cost extra?

Adding a team section to an existing page is a standard update and is covered by your plan. Building a dedicated standalone team page counts as an additional page at the standard $199 fee. See Can you add more pages?

What to send us

Collect profile photos (at least 400x400px, ideally square) and a short bio or bullet points for each person. Submit everything through the client portal with a note about where you'd like the team section to appear.
