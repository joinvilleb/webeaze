What emails will I receive after subscribing for a new website?

Last updated: 05-20-2026    1 min read

Welcome and invite to the client portal

After subscribing to WebEaze,you'll receive a welcome email. In addition, you'll receive an invite email to set your password for your Client Portal.

Good to know: When submitting your Content Submission form from your portal, bullet points and rough notes are fine. You don't need polished copy to get started. See the content checklist for a full breakdown of what to prepare.

Your website is live!

You'll see the build progress in your portal. Once your website is approved and published, you'll receive a final email confirming that your site is live and accessible at your domain.

Please review the link at your earliest convenience. If you don't like what you see, we can take it down temporarily while we revise it. See the project timeline breakdown for a detailed breakdown of the entire launch process.

Propagation is usually complete within a few minutes, but can take up to 24 hours in rare cases.
