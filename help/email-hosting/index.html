Email hosting costs

Last updated: 08-04-2026    1 min read

We don't cover email hosting costs as part of your plan.

I need a business email address

For business email addresses (like hello@yourbusiness.com), you'll need a email hosting service. Most common options are:

  • Google Workspace
  • Microsoft 365
  • Zoho Mail

Can you help me set it up?

Yes. Once you sign up with an email hosting service, we'll update your DNS records to connect your domain to your email. See our custom email setup guide.
