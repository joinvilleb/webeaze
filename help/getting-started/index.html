What is WebEaze?

WebEaze (we, us) is a website development and management service designed specifically for small businesses. We build your website, host it, and optimize it for one low monthly price. You don't need to know anything about web development as it's all handled for you.

Think of it like having a web team on call, while we help drive your business forward. Need to update your hours? Change a photo? Add a new service? Need more leads? Send a message and it's taken care of.

What's included with WebEaze?

  • A custom website: designed to match your business, not a generic template
  • Hosting included: your site lives on our servers, no extra costs
  • Unlimited updates: text, images, prices, hours - just ask and it's done.
  • Ongoing support: real people who know your site and can actually help
  • SEO basics: your site is set up so search engines can find it
  • No long-term contracts: month to month, cancel whenever you want

How does it start?

The first step is a free discovery call. We'll look at your business, ask a few questions, and show you examples of what your site could look like. There's no charge or obligations and helps us see if we're a good fit for each other.

Then what happens?

  1. Send us your content: your logo, business info, photos, and anything else you'd like on the site. All done in one place.
  2. We build your site: a design and develops it based on what you sent. Most websites are ready within 5 to 14 days.
  3. You review it: a link is sent to you to review your website. If anything needs changing, you get up to 3 rounds of revisions.
  4. We launch it: once you're happy, we connect your domain, do our final review, and launch the site.
  5. Always up-to-date: we're here after launch. Any time you need an update, just send a request and it's done, no extra charges.

Ok, so how much?

There's a one-time setup fee + your monthly plan. See our pricing page for exact amounts, and to find the right fit for your business.

Who is this for?

We work well for small businesses that need a professional website without the hassle of managing it themselves. Think service businesses, restaurants, contractors, consultants, local shops, and more. If you want a website that just works and someone to call when it doesn't, we've got you covered.
