Can I pause my plan?

Last updated: 07-21-2026    2 min read

No. Your plan is actively maintained even when you're not even requesting updates.

If you need a break

If you're going through a slow season, a life change, or just don't have updates to submit right now, your plan is still helping you: hosting, security monitoring, uptime checks, and backups are still handled for you.

What you can do instead

  • Cancel and reactivate later: You can cancel anytime and come back when you're ready. Please note, your previous billing date won't carry over, and a new setup process may be required if files have been removed. See what you keep when you cancel.
  • Queue requests for later: You don't have to use updates every month. If you have upcoming changes you'd like to make later, use Support Schedules to set them up.

If you're experiencing financial hardship with your business, we want to help. Contact us and we'll review your situation on a case-by-case basis.
