What are support schedules?

Last updated: 02-01-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • How it works
  • Perfect for
  • Why clients love it

Support schedules let you plan website changes in advance. Tell us what you want updated and when, and we'll handle it automatically so you don't have to remember to reach out when the time comes.

How it works

  1. Let us know ahead of time: Submit your request with the content changes and your preferred publish date
  2. We prepare everything: We'll build and stage the updates before your deadline
  3. Changes go live on schedule: Your updates are published exactly when you need them

Perfect for

  • Seasonal promotions: Schedule your holiday sales, summer specials, or back-to-school offers weeks in advance
  • Event announcements: Plan event pages to go live at a specific date and time
  • Price changes: Update pricing on a set date without last-minute requests
  • Service updates: Add new services or update hours when they take effect
  • Content refreshes: Plan regular content updates like testimonials, portfolio pieces, or blog posts

Why clients love it

Running a business is busy. Support schedules mean you can batch your website planning when it's convenient, then forget about it. No more sticky notes, calendar reminders, or last-minute panic emails. Just tell us once, and it's done.

Included with all plans: Support schedules are available to all WebEaze clients at no extra cost. Simply mention your preferred date when submitting a request.
