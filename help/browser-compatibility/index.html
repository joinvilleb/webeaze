Will my website work on all browsers and devices?

Last updated: 04-10-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • Browsers we test
  • Devices we test
  • What we don't test
  • If something looks off on your device

Yes. Every website we build is tested across major browsers and screen sizes before it goes live. Your site will work correctly for the vast majority of your visitors regardless of how they access it.

Browsers we test

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari (desktop and iOS)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

Devices we test

  • Desktop and laptop screens
  • Tablets
  • Mobile phones (iOS and Android)

What we don't test

We don't test on outdated browsers such as Internet Explorer or very old versions of any browser. These browsers represent a very small share of web traffic and have technical limitations that aren't worth engineering around for most small businesses.

If something looks off on your device

If you spot a layout issue or something that doesn't render correctly, submit a request with a screenshot and the browser or device you're using. We'll investigate and fix it.
