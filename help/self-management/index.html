Can I manage my own website?

Last updated: 04-11-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What you can manage
  • What you shouldn't manage
  • Why this matters

We provide ongoing updates and maintenance to keep your website professional, secure, and high performing. We encourage you to focus on your business while we handle the technical aspects. However, we understand some clients may want to make minor updates themselves.

What you can manage

  • Minor text changes. E.g., correcting typos, updating service descriptions, updating business hours, contact info
  • Adding/removing products (if an eCommerce site)
  • Adding blog posts or news updates
  • Adding links to your website and associated service pages
  • Updating images or media content

What you shouldn't manage

  • Editing products in your store (if an eCommerce site)
  • Installing plugins, integrations, or apps
  • Editing the website's code. Includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Changing any settings related to hosting, domains, or DNS
  • Adjusting layouts or mobile design elements

Why this matters

Your website is built and optimized according to best practices for performance, security, and SEO. Unauthorized changes can inadvertently introduce issues that affect functionality, user experience, or search rankings. Our request page is available at any time for you to request updates or changes you need. Please use it as often as needed to avoid potential issues.

Important: Unauthorized changes may lead to design inconsistencies, security vulnerabilities, or functionality issues. If you make changes outside of our support, it may be rejected by us or charged a fee to resolve it. We're not responsible for changes not completed by WebEaze.
