What Is the WebEaze Client Portal?

Last updated: 08-14-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • What you can do in the portal
  • Why it matters
  • Do I need any technical skills?
  • How do I get access?

The WebEaze client portal is a private dashboard where every WebEaze client requests website changes, tracks their status, sees live performance and leads, and manages their plan, all in one place. It replaces the email-and-invoice back-and-forth that most web design agencies rely on.

When you hire a typical agency or freelancer, a change usually means an email, a wait, and a separate bill. WebEaze works differently. Because we build, host, and manage your site on one flat monthly plan, everything lives in your portal at portal.webeaze.io, so you always know what is happening with your website.

What you can do in the portal

  • Request website changes from a list of common updates, then track every request from received to completed.
  • See your live site report, with real Google Search Console data on impressions, clicks, and rankings. More on the site report.
  • Track your leads from your website's contact forms in one place. More on leads.
  • Read notes from your team and keep your own.
  • Manage your plan and billing, and earn rewards for referrals.

Why it matters

A managed website is only as good as how easy it is to keep current. The portal means you never chase anyone for a small change, wonder how your site is doing, or dig through email for what was done. You ask, we handle it, and you see the results.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. The portal is built for busy business owners, not developers. If you can send an email, you can use it, and a real person on our team handles the actual work on your site.

How do I get access?

Every WebEaze client gets a portal invite when their site is ready. Not a client yet? Book a free preview and we'll show you your new site and the portal before you pay anything. Want the full tour first? Read the Client Portal walkthrough.
