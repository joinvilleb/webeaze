Setting up a custom email address

Last updated: 07-23-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • How custom email works
  • Popular email providers
  • What we can help with
  • Get started

A professional email address like you@yourbusiness.com looks more credible than a free Gmail or Yahoo address. It builds trust with customers and provides more resources longer term..

How custom email works

It uses your domain name (e.g., yourbusiness.com) to create a personalized email addresses. The email is typically hosted by a third-party provider. We typically work with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or Zoho Mail.

Popular email providers

We typically work with these providers. Visit their sites for current pricing and features:

  • Google Workspace
  • Microsoft 365
  • Zoho Mail
  • Hostinger Email
Important: Email hosting costs aren't covered by us. These are separate plans billed directly by the provider.

What we can help with

We generally don't cover email hosting costs, but we can:

  • Configure DNS records: Set up MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to connect your domain to your email provider
  • Guide you through setup: Walk you through the process of creating your email accounts
  • Troubleshoot issues: Help resolve problems with email not sending or receiving

Get started

  1. Choose an email provider that fits your needs and budget
  2. Sign up for their service and verify your domain ownership
  3. Submit a request for us to configure your DNS records
  4. Create your email accounts in your provider's dashboard
Tip: DNS changes typically take 1-24 hours to propagate. If your email isn't working immediately after setup, give it some time before troubleshooting.
