What Are Geo Pages?

Last updated: 02-21-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • Examples of Geo Pages
  • Are Geo Pages included in my plan?
  • Why geo pages matter

If you offer services in multiple cities or regions, geo pages can help your business appear in local search results for each area. These location-specific pages are optimized to target keywords relevant to the services you provide in those locations, even if you don't have a physical office there.

Examples of Geo Pages

  • "Plumbing services in Salisbury, MD"
  • "Landscaping in Trenton, NJ"
  • "HVAC repair in Philadelphia, PA"
  • "Personal training in Wilmington, DE"

Each geo page is custom-written to highlight your services in that specific city, helping local customers find you online.

Are Geo Pages included in my plan?

Your plan includes a set number of pages that you can use for either main pages or geo pages. If you need more pages beyond your plan's limit, we can add them for a one-time fee. There's more information about how additional pages work here.

Why geo pages matter

They're good for SEO, local visibility, and attracting customers in areas where you want to grow your business.
