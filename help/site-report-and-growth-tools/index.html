Your Site Report and Growth Tools

Last updated: 08-14-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • Search performance
  • Site speed and reviews
  • Review radar
  • Compare locally
  • Worth doing next
  • Business tools

Your Client Portal includes a live site report and a set of growth tools that show how your website is performing. Plus, you'll find insights into where your next customers will come from, and more.

Access it to go to your Client Portal  >  > Your site report.

Search performance

You'll see live data: how often your site shows up in search, how many people click, and where you typically rank. The quickest way to know if you are getting found.

Site speed and reviews

A quick read on how fast your site loads, next to your latest customer reviews, so you can see the two things visitors judge you on at a glance.

Review radar

We keep an eye on your Google reviews so new ones do not slip by. Staying on top of reviews is one of the fastest ways to win more local trust.

Compare locally

See how you stack up against similar businesses nearby, so you know where you lead and where there is room to catch up.

Worth doing next

One suggestion at a time, drawn from your own site and search data, plus seasonal ideas worth acting on around now. We put the most valuable one in front of you rather than handing you a list to sort through. Choose What we would do to see exactly what we would change, then Yes, do this for me to file it as a request, or Not for me to bring up the next one.

Business tools

A handful of practical extras built into your portal, including a QR code generator that points customers straight to your site or reviews.

More features are coming soon!
