Can you add a Google Map to my website?

Last updated: 05-26-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What can be embedded
  • How to request it
  • Does it cost extra?

Yes. An embedded Google Map is a great addition for any business where location matters — brick-and-mortar shops, service businesses with a physical address, or any business that wants to show their service area visually.

What can be embedded

  • Your business location — A pinned map showing your address. Visitors can click it to get directions directly in Google Maps.
  • Your Google Business Profile listing — Shows your hours, reviews, and contact info alongside the map pin.
  • A service area map — A shaded region or custom map showing the geographic area you cover (useful for contractors, cleaners, delivery businesses).

How to request it

Submit a request via the client portal and include:

  • Your business address (or the address you want pinned)
  • Where on the page you'd like the map (e.g. Contact page, footer, About page)
  • Whether you want just a map or the full Google Business Profile embed

We'll generate the embed code and add it to your site, usually within 1–2 business days.

Does it cost extra?

Adding a basic Google Maps embed is a standard update and is covered under your plan's unlimited updates. Custom interactive maps or multi-location map builds may involve additional scope.
