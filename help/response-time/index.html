How quickly will you respond to my messages?

Last updated: 04-25-2026    1 min read

On this page

  • Response times by request type
  • Business hours
  • Response vs. completion

We typically respond to all requests and messages within one business day. Here is a more detailed breakdown.

Response times by request type

Standard update requests
Within 1 business day
Questions and general inquiries
Within 1 business day
Urgent issues (site down, critical error)
Within a few hours during business hours
After-hours and weekend submissions
Next business day

Business hours

Our team operates Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern Time. Requests submitted outside those hours are handled when we reopen. We're closed on major US holidays.

Response vs. completion

A response means we have acknowledged your request and it's in the queue. Completion time depends on what was requested. See When will my request be completed? for specific completion timelines by request type.

Tip: Add support@webeaze.io to your contacts so our replies don't go to spam.
