Can I add a blog to my website?

Last updated: 02-24-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What a blog can do for your business
  • How it works
  • Is a blog included in my plan?

Yes, we can add a blog or news section to your website. A blog is a great way to share updates, build trust with your audience, and improve your search engine rankings.

What a blog can do for your business

  • Improve SEO: Fresh, relevant content helps your site rank higher on Google
  • Build credibility: Share your expertise and establish yourself as an authority in your field
  • Engage customers: Keep visitors coming back with news, tips, and updates
  • Drive traffic: Blog posts can be shared on social media to attract new visitors

How it works

We design a blog page that matches your website and set it up so posts are easy to manage. You can:

  • Send us the content: Write your posts and submit them through the client portal. We'll format and publish them for you.
  • Manage it yourself: If your site is on a platform like WordPress, you can add and edit posts directly from your dashboard.

Is a blog included in my plan?

A blog page counts toward your plan's page limit. If you're on the Essential plan (up to 3 pages), adding a blog uses one of those pages. Need more pages? See Can you add more pages? for pricing.
