I don't want a monthly plan. What are my options?

Last updated: 07-23-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • One-Time Website Help
  • Subscribe later
  • Custom Quotes
  • Eligibility

One-Time Website Help

We offer one-time websites as an alternative to a monthly plan. You pay once for what you need, and we deliver it without an ongoing plan. You can learn more about one-time website help and offerings here.

Tip: If you decide to subscribe after the one-time website help, your setup fee and 1st month are waived. Learn more.

Since there's no ongoing plan, the following services aren't part of a one-time service:

  • Ongoing updates or change requests after delivery
  • Hosting, SSL, or backups
  • SEO monitoring or performance reports
  • Support after the website/update is complete

For full website builds, the files and credentials are handed off to you at delivery.

Subscribe later

You can start with a one-time website help and subscribe later. There's no deadline or obligation to subscribe after you pay once, though the 14-day discount window described above does expire.

Custom Quotes

Have specific needs that don't fit our plans/tiers? Contact us for a custom quote.

Eligibility

If you're a current subscriber, you're not eligible for one-time website help. It's only available to new clients who haven't subscribed in the past.
