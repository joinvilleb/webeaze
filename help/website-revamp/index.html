Can I get my website redesigned or revamped?

Last updated: 04-14-2026    3 min read

On this page

  • Is a revamp included in my plan?
  • What's included in a revamp
  • What's not included
  • How to submit content for a revamp
  • How to get a revamp

Routine content updates — text changes, new photos, layout tweaks, adding sections — are included in every WebEaze plan and we'll take care of those at no extra cost. A website revamp is something different. It means we'll rebuild your site's design from the ground up: new visual direction, new layout structure, updated typography, and a refreshed overall look and feel.

Is a revamp included in my plan?

No. A full website revamp isn't included in any plan, including the Growth Plan. Plans cover ongoing management, updates, and maintenance of your existing site. A revamp is a separate, one-time project because it requires the same level of work as building a new website.

What's included in a revamp

  • A complete redesign of your existing site's visual identity and layout
  • Updated structure, typography, and color scheme
  • All existing content migrated to the new design
  • Mobile responsiveness and performance optimization
  • Up to 3 pages redesigned (additional pages available at the standard per-page rate)

What's not included

  • New pages beyond the 3-page limit (see Additional Pages pricing)
  • New written content or copywriting
  • Brand identity or logo design
  • E-commerce setup (available as a separate add-on)

How to submit content for a revamp

Submit everything through the content submission form. Here are some ideas of what will help us:

  • Brand colors: hex codes or a description of your preferred colors. If you don't have any, describe the vibe so we can tailor it for you.
  • Page content: any updated text for your pages. If you're keeping the existing copy, enter "keep current" for that field.
  • Photos: upload any images you'd like used. If you don't have any, enter N/A and we'll use professional stock imagery.
  • Inspiration sites: links to websites you like the look of. If nothing comes to mind, enter N/A.

How to get a revamp

Purchase the revamp directly below. Once your payment is confirmed, send us a message through your client portal letting us know you'd like a full redesign and describing what you want to change. We'll review your current site, confirm the scope, and get started. You'll see a preview before anything goes live.

Good to know: The $399 revamp fee is a one-time charge on top of your monthly plan. It won't affect your subscription or billing cycle.
