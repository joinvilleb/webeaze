How does the free discovery call work?

Last updated: 04-25-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What happens on the call?
  • Do I have to book a call?
  • What if I skip it?

The free discovery call is a free, no-obligation 15-minute call with our team before you subscribe. It's designed to make sure we're a good fit and that we fully understand your project before any work begins.

What happens on the call?

  • We'll learn about your business, goals, and the kind of website you have in mind
  • We'll answer any questions you have about our plans, process, or timeline
  • We'll confirm whether we can build what you're looking for within your plan's scope
  • You'll get a clear picture of what to expect before you commit to anything

Do I have to book a call?

No, it's optional. You can subscribe directly from the pricing page without a call. However, we strongly recommend it — especially if you have specific features in mind or want to confirm scope before paying.

What if I skip it?

If you subscribe without a call and your project turns out to be more complex than our standard plans cover, we'll reach out to discuss options. In very rare cases, we may not be able to fulfill the project. The call helps prevent that.
