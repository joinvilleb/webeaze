Can you help with Yelp, TripAdvisor, or other review platforms?

Last updated: 05-30-2026    2 min read

On this page

  • What we can do
  • What we don't manage
  • Platforms by industry
  • Where to focus first

Google is our primary focus for local SEO because it has the biggest impact on search rankings. But we know other platforms matter depending on your industry, and there are ways we can help.

What we can do

  • Add review platform links or badges (Yelp, TripAdvisor, etc.) to your website
  • Display your rating or recent reviews on your site where technically supported
  • Add a "Leave us a review" prompt that points to any platform you choose
  • Help you write a professional response to negative reviews on any platform

What we don't manage

We don't manage your Yelp or TripAdvisor listings directly. Those platforms require you to claim and maintain your own business profile. We also can't remove or flag reviews on platforms outside Google.

Platforms by industry

  • Restaurants and hospitality: Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable
  • Home services: Houzz, Angi, HomeAdvisor
  • Healthcare: Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals
  • General local businesses: Facebook Reviews, Better Business Bureau

Where to focus first

Start with Google reviews. They have the most direct impact on how you rank in Search and Maps. Once your Google presence is solid, expand to whichever platform your customers use most.
