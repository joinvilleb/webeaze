How it all comes together

Our Process

We turn your idea into a live website with a clear, human process. You do not need to learn tools. We guide you at each step.

See the steps Compare plans
Kickoff Setup Design Launch
1

Kickoff call

We meet for about fifteen minutes to learn your goals and pages. Share any logos, photos, or text you have. If you do not have content yet, we help you plan it.

  • Pick pages and features
  • Choose a style direction
  • Confirm a draft date
Book your kickoff
2

Setup and structure

We set up your domain and hosting, then build a clean base with your pages and menu. You see a simple layout before we add design.

  • Domain, DNS, and SSL set
  • Pages and navigation ready
  • Basic analytics connected
3

Design and first draft

We add your colors, fonts, and content. You get a live preview link. Leave comments right on the page. We apply your notes and polish.

  • Brand styling added
  • Real content placed
  • Feedback rounds kept simple
4

Launch and ongoing care

We go live after your final check. From there you get unlimited updates and friendly help. We keep an eye on speed and basic SEO.

  • Forms and links checked
  • Google Business help if needed
  • Future changes included

Quick answers

How long does it take
The first draft often arrives in a few days. Typical launches are one to two weeks depending on content.
What do you need from me
Any logos, photos, or text you have. If you do not have content, we help you outline it step by step.
Can you work with my current site
Yes. We can redesign, migrate, or improve what you have. We will suggest the safest path.
