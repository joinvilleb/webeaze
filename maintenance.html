FAQ

Questions about maintenance

Is it really unlimited? What's the catch?

No catch. Send as many requests as you need. Includes content changes, photo swaps, new pages, fixes. We don't have "fair use" limit caps*. Our model is built around keeping you happy long-term.

What platforms do you support?

WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom HTML/CSS, and more. See our supported platforms for the full list.

How fast are updates completed?

Most updates are done within 24-48 hours, often same-day. Urgent issues (site down, broken forms) are prioritized immediately at no extra cost.

Can you work on my existing site?

Yes. We improve design, speed, and fix issues on sites you already have. Learn more about our existing site management.

What if I need something complex?

Custom features like booking systems or e-commerce integrations may have one-time development costs. We're transparent upfront. Regular maintenance is always included.
