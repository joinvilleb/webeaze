Is it really unlimited? What's the catch?

Yes — truly unlimited.1 Submit as many requests as you need. Content changes, photo swaps, new pages, and bug fixes, all included. We do have a fair use policy to protect service quality for all clients (up to 10 requests and 10 hours of work per month), but the vast majority of clients never come close to it.

How do I actually submit a request?

You can submit a request through our online request form, reply to any email from our team, schedule a call if it's easier to explain verbally, or record a quick screen video. We accept requests in whatever format is easiest for you. Just describe what you want changed.

How fast are updates completed?

Most requests are completed within 24–48 hours, and many are done same-day. Urgent issues like a downed site, broken contact form, or something affecting lead generation are treated as emergencies and prioritized immediately at no extra cost.

Can you work on my existing website?

Yes. WebEaze can take over management of your existing site regardless of platform: WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom HTML, and more. We'll review your current setup and handle ongoing maintenance from there.

What if I need something more complex, like a booking system?

Complex new features like booking integrations, e-commerce, or custom calculators may involve a one-time development cost depending on the scope. We'll always give you a clear estimate before doing anything. Routine maintenance, updates, and fixes are always covered in your plan.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel?

No contract. WebEaze is month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no fees or penalties. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.
