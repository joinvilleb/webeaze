Questions? Answered.

Do I keep ownership of my profile?
Yes, always. You own the profile. We're added as managers and can be removed at any time. Your business, your control.
How often do you update my profile?
We review and update your profile monthly, publishing posts and photos tied to your promotions. Need an urgent update? Submit a support request.
Do you support multiple locations?
Yes. We manage each location under your brand. First location is included with Growth & Elite plans; additional locations are $39/month each.
Will you respond to my reviews?
We provide suggested replies written in your tone. Full reply management is available on Growth and Elite plans—just submit a request when you get a new review.
What if I already have a Google Business Profile?
Perfect. We'll send a manager invite to your existing profile. Once you approve, we'll audit it and optimize everything.
How do I get started?
If you're on Growth or Elite, just submit a request to activate. On Essential? Add the feature through your billing portal or contact us.
