Any questions?

Do I need to know anything about websites?
No. You'll provide basic info about your business. We'll handle the entire tech side from design to launch to ongoing maintenance.
What's included in the monthly price?
Website design, hosting, SSL, domain management, unlimited content updates, SEO, security monitoring, and support.
How fast can my site go live?
Once you approve your mockup, your site goes live within 5-14 business days. The free mockup is provided within 48 hours of your first call with us.
What if I already have a website?
We've got you covered. We can take control of your existing website (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and more) or rebuild it from scratch. More information about this can be found here.
Is there a contract?
No. Plans are billed monthly or annually. You can cancel at any time with no notice period. We're confident you won't want to. 😉
Will my website show up in AI search like ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity?
Our Growth plan includes Answer Engine Optimization (AEO). It includes structured data markup, FAQ schema, and llms.txt files specifically designed to help AI tools understand and recommend your business.
Do you serve businesses outside the Mid-Atlantic?
Yes. All work is done remotely, so we work with small businesses across all 50 US states. We do have a strong presence in Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Washington D.C.
How much does a small business website cost?
There's no big upfront bill. You pay a one-time $199 setup, then a flat monthly plan starting at $169. That covers custom design, hosting, unlimited content updates, SEO, security, and support, with no hourly rates and no surprise fees. Many agencies charge thousands upfront for a site you then have to maintain yourself.
Do I own my website and domain?
Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and your content always belongs to you. We build, host, and manage everything while you're a client, and if you ever decide to leave, we help you transition smoothly.
How is this different from Wix, Squarespace, or hiring a freelancer?
With a DIY builder like Wix or Squarespace, you're still the one designing, writing, updating, and troubleshooting. A freelancer usually builds once and moves on. We do all of it for you on an ongoing basis: design, hosting, unlimited updates, SEO, and support, for one predictable monthly price.
Will you help me get found on Google in my local area?
Yes. Every site is built with local SEO in mind, and we manage your Google Business Profile so you show up when nearby customers search for what you do. Our Growth plan adds Answer Engine Optimization so AI tools can recommend you too.
What if I want changes after my website launches?
Just ask. Content updates are unlimited and included in every plan. Request a change from your portal, whether it's new photos, hours, pricing, or a whole new page, and we take care of it. No extra invoices.
What if I don't like the free preview?
Then you walk away at no cost and with no pressure. We design a free mockup of your new site within 48 hours of your first call, so you see the finished look before you ever pay. You only move forward if you love it.
Do you work with businesses in my industry?
Most likely, yes. We focus on local service businesses like trades, gyms, restaurants, clinics, salons, and contractors. See recent work with Galaxy Gymnastics, Clam Tavern, and Ibis Prep on our samples page.
