Everything you should know.

How much does a website cost from WebEaze? +

We offer monthly and annual plans. Monthly plans start at $169/month. Since we're offering a website at a very affordable price, there's a one-time $199 setup fee. Check our pricing page for more information.

How long does it take to build my website? +

After your free discovery call, we share a design direction within 48 hours. Once you review and approve it, most websites launch within 1 to 2 weeks. Having your content ready helps us move faster.

What's included in a WebEaze plan? +

Every plan includes custom website design, hosting, SSL, unlimited content updates, mobile optimization, basic SEO, and email support. We offer two plans, Essential and Growth. The Growth plan adds priority turnaround, phone and video support, Google Business Profile management, advanced local SEO, and review management.

Do I need to sign a contract? +

There's no contract. WebEaze plans are month-to-month. You're welcome to cancel anytime with no hassle. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.

Can you manage my existing website? +

Yes. We can take over an existing website. We work with platforms likeWordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom HTML, and more. You don't need a brand new website to get started. Please check out our supported providers here.

Where does WebEaze serve businesses? +

We serve small businesses across the United States, with a strong presence in the Mid-Atlantic: Delaware, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Virginia, and Washington D.C.

Does WebEaze help my business show up on Google? +

Yes. Every plan includes on-page SEO setup: page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image optimization, and site speed improvements. The Growth plan adds advanced local SEO and Google Business Profile management, which helps your business show up in Google Search and Google Maps when people search for what you offer near them.

What makes WebEaze different from other web designers? +

WebEaze is not just a build-and-handoff service. After your site launches, everything is handled for you on a monthly basis: updates, hosting, SSL, security, and support are all included at a flat rate. You never need to call a separate hosting company, hire a freelancer for changes, or deal with technical issues. We run the tech so you can run your business.
